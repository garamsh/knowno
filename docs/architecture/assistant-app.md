# Assistant app

The app's `assistant` domain: the chat with 말랑이 on the phone, its client of the `assistant` function, the conversation kept on the device, the proposals the user adds, the photos it reads, 홈's chat bubble, and how 홈 opens it. The function's contract is [`assistant.md`](assistant.md)'s; the web's chat is [`web-assistant.md`](web-assistant.md)'s.

## Current decisions

### Surface and location

- The domain lives in `app/lib/assistant/`; its surface is `app/lib/assistant/assistant.dart`, which exports `AssistantChatScreen` and `OpenNote`, `AssistantClient`, `HttpAssistantClient` and `assistantUrl`, and the providers `assistantClientProvider`, `assistantStorageProvider`, `assistantPhotoSourceProvider` `assistantReadyCardsProvider`, `assistantInviteProvider` with `ChatInvite` and `assistantClockProvider`, with `assistantStorageKeys` for the composition site ([ADR 0201](adr/0201-chat-with-malangi-on-the-phone-in-an-assistant-domain-kept-in-the-app-s-preferences-and-opened-from-home-s-mascot.md), [`structure.md`](structure.md)). Its internals are under `app/lib/assistant/src/`:
  - `protocol.dart` — the stream's events, proposals, choices and refusals as sealed types, parsed at the boundary, and the share of the day's tokens left;
  - `event_stream.dart` — the `text/event-stream` reader;
  - `assistant_client.dart` — `AssistantClient`, the seam, and `HttpAssistantClient`, which streams the message, commits a proposal and reads `assistant_allowance()`;
  - `conversation.dart` — the turns, a reply's events applied, the last 40 turns a message sends, the ready proposal, and the stored form;
  - `assistant_controller.dart` — `AssistantController`, the one notifier that sends, retries, stops, commits and clears, and keeps the conversation in the app's preferences;
  - `providers.dart` — the providers above, and `ChatInvite`, the day 홈's bubble has shown;
  - `chat_screen.dart` — the chat; `reply_markdown.dart` — a reply's text as Markdown; `proposal_view.dart` — a proposal's groups and cards; `card_preview.dart` — a proposed card in two lines, a text of version 3 as its plain text and a cloze of version 2 as `___` with its hint on the front and its answers on the back, read through `deck`'s `markupEngine` ([ADR 0207](adr/0207-render-and-edit-card-text-markup-and-cloze-in-the-app-through-the-engine-behind-a-deck-owned-seam-and-write-text-version-3.md));
  - `supabase_photo_store.dart` — `SupabasePhotoStore`, the private `assistant-photos` bucket through Supabase Storage.
- The domain imports `photo_import` through its surface, for the camera, the library and the JPEG a photo is prepared as, and `supabase_flutter` for the storage adapter, `markdown` for a reply's text, `flutter_timezone` for the zone name, `deck`'s surface for the markup engine its card preview reads a card's text through ([ADR 0207](adr/0207-render-and-edit-card-text-markup-and-cloze-in-the-app-through-the-engine-behind-a-deck-owned-seam-and-write-text-version-3.md)), and no other domain ([ADR 0204](adr/0204-draw-malangi-at-a-screen-s-edge-name-the-note-a-chat-opens-from-attach-photos-to-a-message-and-follow-their-job-from-the-phone.md)). `home/` opens it ([`home.md`](home.md)); the composition site builds `HttpAssistantClient` from the Supabase project and passes it to `composeApp` as `assistant`, which hides every way into the chat when it is null.

### The conversation

- One session, never a list: a user's message and 말랑이's reply are turns, and a reply holds its text, its sources, its proposals, its question and how it ended (whole, failed at the model, cut at the output limit, or stopped by the user).
- It is kept in the app's preferences, `assistant.conversation` for the turns and `assistant.draft` for the message being written, so typing rewrites no turn. A reply cut off by the app's closing is read back as stopped. A text that is no conversation reads as the empty one.
- A message sends the last 40 turns, each reply with the proposals it showed and the question it asked ([`assistant.md`](assistant.md) §Requests). A user's message is at most 4,000 characters, as the function's.
- "대화 지우기", in the chat's ⋯, asks "대화를 지울까요?" and clears the turns and the draft; it is not offered while a reply streams.
- The notifier outlives the screen, so 홈's bubble reads what the chat draws. Leaving the chat stops a reply still streaming, and opening a note over it does not.

### The stream and its refusals

- A message is `POST /functions/v1/assistant` with the session's token, answered as `text/event-stream`; `text`, `interim`, `choice`, `proposal`, `citation`, `job`, `error` and `usage` are read, and an event of another name is dropped. A known event whose data is not its form ends the reply as failed. The `job` event starts a photo job's line under the reply (§Photos and their job).
- `usage` brings the day's tokens, and the share left, "오늘 n% 남았어요", sits under the chat's title; it is read through `assistant_allowance()` when the chat opens, and not shown while it cannot be read.
- A refusal is told by its status and body: `429` `rate_limited` (the budget is used: the mascot sleeps and the field goes), `429` `busy` (another message still runs: the message is sent again after 3 s, up to three times, and then asks "다시 시도"), `401`, `403`, `413`, and any other status or a connection that fails.
- A reply's text sits in a bubble at the start of the line, as the user's is at its end, and is drawn as Markdown with GFM: headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, inline and fenced code, nested lists, block quotes, rules and tables. `markdown` parses it and `reply_markdown.dart` draws it from the theme's tokens ([ADR 0225](adr/0225-draw-malangi-s-reply-as-markdown-with-gfm-in-a-bubble-through-the-dart-markdown-package-and-our-own-widgets.md)). A link with the scheme `http`, `https` or `mailto` opens in the system browser, as a citation does, and any other scheme is text; an image is drawn as its alt text and never fetched; raw HTML is text; a table and a code block wider than the bubble scroll sideways inside it. The bubble is named "말랑이" and keeps its content's own semantics: the text reads without marks, and a link is a node a screen reader can activate. The parser reads every text, so there is no fallback. While a reply streams the whole text is read again on each change, so a mark not yet closed shows as typed until it closes. A user's turn stays plain, and the proposal, question, job and sources stay below the bubble, not in it.
- The function's `interim` event says every `text` since the previous one, or since the turn began, was interim ([`assistant.md`](assistant.md) §Stream, ADR 0224). The turn then holds that text as one line, `AssistantTurn.interim`, with its whitespace folded to single spaces, and its `text` starts again: each new interim replaces the line, and the line goes when the reply's text begins or the turn ends. It is drawn under the turn in the place of "말랑이가 생각하는 중이에요", in the same polite live region, cut to one line and read with the reply's parser and extension set (ADR 0225) as its words without marks (`plainLine`: bold, italic, strikethrough and code as their text, a link as its text, an image as its alt text), so a screen reader reads it when it changes and not on each delta. A reply's leading whitespace is dropped, which drops the separator the function sends before a call's text. The line is never stored and never sent back, so a stored or sent turn holds the reply alone; a turn of one call has no interim and is drawn as before.
- A reply that failed offers "다시 시도", which replaces it; one cut at the output limit says so; "멈추기" stops one, keeping what arrived.
- A turn whose `usage` says `depth_limit` or `time_limit` ([`assistant.md`](assistant.md) §Stream) and which holds no reply, proposal, question or job ends as `limit`: the notice "할 일이 많아 여기서 멈췄어요. 다시 시도해 주세요." with "다시 시도", which replaces it as it does a failed reply. A limit turn that holds any of them is drawn as that alone, and says nothing of the limit. `usage` is last, so the notice appears when the stream ends, in the place of the passing line. The ending is stored with the turn.

### Proposals

- A proposal arrives whole, and again whole under its id when a group is remade; each group that kept its cards keeps its checks, and a remade one is checked whole.
- It is drawn as one group on the surface: the note ("새 노트 · name" for a new one), then each group with its check, its title and its form chip, "다른 형식으로", which offers the other forms but a photo's and the group's own and sends "title 부분은 form 형식으로 다시 만들어 줘", and its cards, each with its check and two lines. A group of six cards or more starts collapsed to "카드 n장 · form".
- The action at the foot is the open proposal's, the last one shown whose reply has ended and which is not added: "n장 추가", or "n장 바꾸기" for changes, over the cards checked, disabled at none. A refused or failed commit says why above it.
- Once added, a proposal loses its checks, and a shortcut under it names the note and its cards, "카드 n장", and, where the commit's `200` also lists cards it did not write ([`assistant.md`](assistant.md) §Commit), the count of each after it: "n장은 이미 노트에 있어요" for the skipped, which the note held already, and "n장은 추가하지 못했어요" for the refused. A commit that writes no card is a `422`, which the refusal above draws. It opens the note through `OpenNote`, which `home/` gives: it runs a sync, waits for it where the note is not on the device, and opens `DeckScreen` over the chat, so back returns to the same turns, scroll and draft.

### The context

- A chat opens from 홈 and names no note ([ADR 0248](adr/0248-meet-malangi-on-home-alone-offer-the-chat-once-a-day-in-a-bubble-over-the-mascot-and-open-it-naming-no-note.md)): `AssistantChatScreen` takes none, adds no line to the thread and sends no `deck_id`. Every message's `context` carries `time_zone`, the device's IANA zone name, which `deviceTimeZone` reads through `flutter_timezone` and `HttpAssistantClient`'s `timeZone` supplies, so the server counts the study day in it ([`assistant.md`](assistant.md) §Requests); where the platform gives no name there is no `context`, and the server counts the day in `Asia/Seoul`, as for a message that names none. A thread stored before ADR 0248 may hold "name 노트에서" lines, each a `ContextTurn`: it loads and is drawn as its line, is never sent as a turn and is not counted in the 40.

### Photos and their job

- The photo button beside the message field, present where `assistantPhotoSourceProvider` holds a source, offers "사진 찍기" and "앨범에서 고르기" and stages up to 50 photos, each a JPEG of at most 6 MiB that `photo_import` prepares; a staged photo is taken out by tapping it. A message may be photos alone.
- A message's photos are uploaded one by one, "사진을 올리는 중이에요", each at `assistant-photos/{user id}/{uuid}` through `HttpAssistantClient`'s `storePhoto`, which is `SupabasePhotoStore`'s `store`: the adapter the domain owns over the Supabase client, which decides the bucket, the path and the refusal where nobody is signed in, and which the composition site only constructs and passes; then the message names them in `photos`. A photo that does not upload sends nothing and leaves the text and the staged photos where they were.
- The `job` event is kept on the reply as a `JobProgress`, which is stored with it. While a job reads, `AssistantController` asks `GET /assistant/jobs/{id}` every 3 s (`jobPollEvery`), from the first event and again after a restart, and draws "n/m장 읽는 중" over a bar, n the photos no longer queued, with "읽기 취소", which calls the cancel route. A job the server does not know ends as cancelled; a poll that fails is asked again.
- When a poll says done and no message has followed the reply, the controller waits out a reply still streaming, then sends a message of its own, `jobDoneMessage`, with the job on the reply that started it (`assistant.md` §Photo jobs, "Composing"); the thread draws no line for it, the job's own line, "사진 n장을 다 읽었어요", says so. The cards made from it are ready like any others. A cancelled job sends nothing.

### 홈

- Where the app has an assistant, 홈's hero mascot is a button named "말랑이와 대화하기" and the first run's primary is "말랑이와 첫 노트 만들기" ([`home.md`](home.md)).
- While a proposal of new cards is ready and not added, a speech bubble over the mascot reads "카드 n장 만들어 뒀어요", n the cards checked; the mascot's name carries it, "말랑이와 대화하기, 카드 n장 만들어 뒀어요". Nothing is shown by default.
- 홈's chat bubble ([ADR 0248](adr/0248-meet-malangi-on-home-alone-offer-the-chat-once-a-day-in-a-bubble-over-the-mascot-and-open-it-naming-no-note.md)) is that bubble's drawing, a surface pill with a tail, centred over the mascot, saying a line of 홈's state: "오늘 배운 거, 같이 카드로 만들까요?" while cards are left today or in a backlog, "수고했어요. 얘기 좀 할래요?" once the day's cards are done, and "말랑이와 대화해요" otherwise. It comes about 1.5 s after 홈 is first the visible screen of a local day, with 홈 the selected tab and no screen over it, stays about 6 s and fades in 0.3 s; a tap opens the chat, as the mascot's does. Under reduce motion it appears and goes without a fade. The ready bubble keeps priority: while it shows, this one does not, and the day is not spent. The day it has shown is `assistant.inviteDay`, a local `yyyy-MM-dd` in the app's preferences, kept per device, and it is spent when the bubble is drawn; `assistantClockProvider` is the clock it reads, which a test substitutes. The bubble is left out of the semantics tree as the ready bubble is, and the mascot's name is unchanged.
- The 노트 tab, a note's screen and 기록 draw no character ([ADR 0248](adr/0248-meet-malangi-on-home-alone-offer-the-chat-once-a-day-in-a-bubble-over-the-mascot-and-open-it-naming-no-note.md)).
