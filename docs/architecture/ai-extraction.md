# AI extraction

The `extract-cards` Edge Function that turns a photo of clear study material, such as a printed word list or glossary, into candidate cards, the material it is for, its limits and privacy, its plans' photo limits, how its model is switched, the eval that chooses the model, and the app's `photo_import` domain that calls it.

## Current decisions

### Goal

- Photo import is for clear material: a page whose entries it lists plainly, such as a printed word list, glossary, bilingual table or dictionary page ([ADR 0128](adr/0128-read-clear-material-in-photo-import-and-send-complex-material-to-the-external-ai-integration.md)).
- The prompt analyses the material and finds what it teaches, its words, terms or facts, and makes a card of each. A list's numbering, ordinals and bullets place an entry and are never a card's front or part of it.
- Complex material, such as handwriting, dense notes and pages marked up in pen, goes to the external-AI integration, where the user works through it with Claude or ChatGPT and adds the cards through the MCP server (#496, [ADR 0126](adr/0126-connect-claude-and-chatgpt-through-one-remote-mcp-server-acting-as-the-signed-in-user.md), [`mcp.md`](mcp.md)). The prompt holds no rule written for it; such a photo is still sent and read like any other.
- #412's research into a two-stage photo import was closed as out of scope on 2026-11-01. The two-stage experiment under §Eval and its report, `docs/eval/two-stage.md`, stay as the record.

### Location

- The function is `supabase/functions/extract-cards/`, a Deno module with no third-party dependency: it calls Supabase Auth, PostgREST and OpenRouter with `fetch` ([ADR 0013](adr/0013-extract-cards-function-and-eval.md)).
- It reads the caller through `supabase/functions/_shared/auth.ts`, which the `delete-account` function shares ([ADR 0026](adr/0026-delete-the-account-through-an-edge-function.md)); a deploy bundles it.
- `index.ts` is its composition site: it reads the configuration once and passes `fetch`, the logger and the clock to the handler, which constructs none of them.
- The eval is `tools/eval/`, outside the function, so the deployed folder holds only the function. It imports the function's extraction module, so it measures the same instructions, prompt and output contract, schema and single call.
- Merging the pull request that changes the function deploys it, through Supabase's GitHub integration; a change needing a new secret or changing the model or the cost of a call merges only after the owner approves it ([`server.md`](server.md) §Edge Function deploys).
- Its secrets are set by hand on the production project, by the owner or the PM only; a merge sets none.

### Contract

- `POST` with the user's access token as `Authorization: Bearer`, and a JSON body:
  - `image` — one JPEG, PNG or WebP image, base64-encoded, at most 6 MiB decoded. The type is read from the image's leading bytes; a declared type is ignored.
  - `languages` — optional `front` and `back` BCP 47 tags, the deck's languages, passed to the model as hints.
  - `cursor` — optional, the `cursor` of an earlier answer for the same photo, sent back with the same image to go on reading it ([ADR 0138](adr/0138-read-a-photo-to-the-end-of-its-page-by-reading-a-cut-reply-on-in-the-request-and-going-on-with-a-cursor-the-photo-counted-once.md)). Such a request is not counted, is read by the model and prompt version of the photo's first read, and answers only the cards after those read, none of them at the page's end.
- Success is `200` with `{ "cards": [...] }`, 1 to 500 cards over a photo's requests, each text field plain trimmed text ([ADR 0051](adr/0051-extract-each-card-with-a-question-a-fullest-and-a-short-answer-and-a-detail-step-and-eval-notes-pages-by-terms.md)):
  - `kind` — `vocabulary` for an entry of a vocabulary list, glossary, bilingual table or dictionary page, `note` for a point of notes;
  - `type` — what the card asks ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md), [ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)): `concept`, a named term, concept, rule or exception and its statement, which every vocabulary card and every judgment card is; `cloze`, a line or sentence of the page with each word to recall written `___`, and its `cloze`; `identify`, an example sentence the page prints with the word to judge in single quotes, and its category and reason; or `table`, a whole table of the page with the cells to recall as blanks, and its `table`. Only a note is a `cloze` or a `table` card;
  - `front` — the headword or phrase as the page prints it, or, for a note, the name of the concept, rule or exception it states, never a question, 1 to 500 characters (code points);
  - `answer` — a note's theory, the definition, rule or exception, in a sentence or a few, 1 to 1000 characters; on a vocabulary card, composed from its senses: one sense's meaning, or one line per sense with its labels, each in brackets, before its meaning; on a cloze card, composed from its answers, one per line; on a table card, composed from its table: the column headers on the first line, then one line per row, cells joined by ` | `;
  - `topic` — the name of the page section the card comes from, its heading without numbering, or the page's title where it has no headings, 1 to 60 characters; cards of one section share it, so an app can group them by it ([ADR 0055](adr/0055-cover-everything-a-notes-page-teaches-at-step-5-with-prompt-v4-and-eval-a-dense-page-by-coverage-per-page.md));
  - `examples` — present only when the page prints any for the card, 1 to 5 example sentences, phrases or worked examples of 1 to 500 characters each; a note's worked examples are those the page prints for its rule;
  - `senses` — on a vocabulary card, its meanings in the page's order, 1 to 10, each `{labels, meaning, examples}` ([ADR 0076](adr/0076-ask-prompt-v8-for-each-sense-s-printed-labels-and-examples-transcribed-never-inferred-and-eval-labels-on-pages-that-print-none.md), the shape [ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md) states for the app):
    - `labels` — 0 to 5, the labels the page prints for that meaning, in its order, transcribed and never inferred, so none where the page prints none, and none from a part of speech printed elsewhere, such as before each reading of a pronunciation: a part of speech `{"kind": "part_of_speech", "category": "<name>", "text": "..."}`, its category one of `noun`, `verb`, `adjective`, `adverb`, `pronoun`, `preposition`, `conjunction`, `interjection`, `determiner`, `numeral`, `particle`, `auxiliary`, `classifier` and `phrase`, or any other label `{"kind": "other", "category": null, "text": "..."}`, such as a semantic class; `text` as printed, without the brackets around it, 1 to 500 characters;
    - `meaning` — the translation or the definition as printed, 1 to 500 characters;
    - `examples` — 0 to 5, the sentences the page prints under that meaning, each 1 to 500 characters;
  - `extras` — on a vocabulary card, present only when the page prints any, 1 to 10 `{label, text}` for anything else it prints for the entry as a whole under its own label, such as 유의어, 반의어 or 파생어, and the grammar the parts of speech do not hold, such as a gender, a plural or a past form, verbatim (ADR 0076);
  - `pronunciation` — on a vocabulary card, present only when the page prints one, verbatim, a pronunciation per part of speech included, such as `명 ['kantent] / 형 [kan'tent]`, 1 to 500 characters;
  - `table` — on a table card only, its table as the page prints it ([ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)): `columns`, 1 to 8 column headers, the top-left corner included; `rows`, 1 to 20 rows of exactly as many cells, each starting with its row header; and `blanks`, the cells to recall, one or more distinct `{row, column}` counted from 0 within `rows`, each on a cell that holds text. A header or cell the page leaves empty is `""`, and each is at most 500 characters;
  - `cloze` — on a cloze card only, `{answers}`: what fills each `___` of its front, in order, 1 to 10 of 1 to 500 characters;
- `partial` — beside the cards, `true` when the page was left unread past them, and `false` when it was read to its end ([ADR 0135](adr/0135-keep-the-cards-a-reply-cut-at-the-output-cap-completed-say-the-photo-was-read-in-part-and-measure-three-ways-to-read-a-dense-page-whole.md), [ADR 0138](adr/0138-read-a-photo-to-the-end-of-its-page-by-reading-a-cut-reply-on-in-the-request-and-going-on-with-a-cursor-the-photo-counted-once.md)). An app built before it ignores it.
- `cursor` — present only when the page was left unread and the photo may go on: an opaque string a client sends back to read on, taken once, within 10 minutes, by the user whose photo it is. A client that ignores it keeps the cards read so far.
- The contract has one version. Until the first store release no older app build is kept working ([ADR 0078](adr/0078-keep-no-older-app-or-function-build-working-until-the-first-store-release.md)), so a change to it deploys with the app built from the same `main`, and an app built before it refuses the new cards until it is rebuilt.
- Every refusal is `{ "error": "<code>" }`, the code naming the case the app shows:

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| 400 | `bad_request` | the body is not JSON, has no base64 `image`, or has a malformed `languages` |
| 401 | `unauthenticated` | no bearer token, an invalid one, the anon key, or an anonymous sign-in |
| 403 | `suspended` | an admin suspended the user ([ADR 0072](adr/0072-suspend-a-user-through-supabase-auth-s-ban-and-restrictive-write-policies-remove-a-public-deck-from-sharing-for-good-and-read-any-deck-in-the-admin-area.md)) |
| 405 | `method_not_allowed` | a method other than `POST` |
| 413 | `too_large` | the image is over 6 MiB decoded, or the body is larger than such an image could make it |
| 415 | `unsupported_type` | the image is not JPEG, PNG or WebP |
| 422 | `no_cards` | the model found no card on the page |
| 410 | `cursor_expired` | the `cursor` is unknown, already taken, past its 10 minutes, or another user's ([ADR 0138](adr/0138-read-a-photo-to-the-end-of-its-page-by-reading-a-cut-reply-on-in-the-request-and-going-on-with-a-cursor-the-photo-counted-once.md)) |
| 429 | `rate_limited` | the user has made the extractions its plan version allows in its period; the body also holds `photo_limit`, that limit, and `period`, `month` or `day` in Asia/Seoul ([ADR 0097](adr/0097-give-each-plan-versions-of-its-terms-with-a-start-free-10-a-month-pro-100-a-day-and-max-unlimited-to-1000-a-day-each-grant-taking-the-latest-at-its-monthly-renewal.md)) |
| 502 | `model_error` | OpenRouter failed, timed out or returned invalid output, on the first call and on its retry where one was made; for a request with a cursor, on each of its calls |
| 503 | `not_configured` | `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` or a platform variable is missing, or the caller's plan has no settings or names a malformed model ID |

- A request is checked in this order: method, configuration, user, body, suspension, the plan with its photo limit, or for a request with a cursor the cursor, model. A refused request never reaches the model, and one refused before the plan never reaches the count.
- `config.toml` sets `verify_jwt = true` for the function, so the platform refuses a request without a valid JWT first. The function then asks Supabase Auth for the token's user, which refuses the anon key, and refuses a user with `is_anonymous`.

### Web client

- The web's `decks` feature reads the same signed-in allowance and calls the existing function through an authenticated Server Action with the current user session; it changes no model, plan, quota or function configuration. Returned cards remain candidates until the user reviews and explicitly adds them ([`web-decks.md`](web-decks.md), [ADR 0122](adr/0122-create-decks-and-reviewed-cards-on-the-web.md)).
- Browser imports accept any image the browser decodes, prepare it as the app does, upright, at most 2048 px and JPEG, under a 3 MiB upload, and stay in the current browser window ([`web-decks.md`](web-decks.md), #506). Verification substitutes extraction responses; no test calls a paid model.

### Model call

- OpenRouter chat completions, with the image as a data URL and `response_format` a strict JSON schema of the card list, generated from the same limits the parser enforces (`cards.ts`).
- `provider.require_parameters` routes only to providers that honour the schema; `max_tokens` is 16384 (`MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS`).
- **A reply cut at the cap** ([ADR 0135](adr/0135-keep-the-cards-a-reply-cut-at-the-output-cap-completed-say-the-photo-was-read-in-part-and-measure-three-ways-to-read-a-dense-page-whole.md)): when OpenRouter reports `finish_reason` `length` and the reply is not whole JSON, it is closed after the last card its JSON completed (`closeCutReply` in `cards.ts`) and those cards are read by the same rules. A cut reply that completed no card is invalid output. A reply with kept cards is not retried.
- **Reading on** ([ADR 0138](adr/0138-read-a-photo-to-the-end-of-its-page-by-reading-a-cut-reply-on-in-the-request-and-going-on-with-a-cursor-the-photo-counted-once.md), `continuation.ts`): after a cut reply that kept cards, the function calls again with the same photo, model and prompt and, after the photo, a text naming how many cards were read and the fronts read, the last of them up to 4,000 characters, asking for every entry of the page not in that list, wherever it is, or an empty list when none is missing. A whole reply of 40 cards or more is read on the same way, to confirm the page ends there, since a model may stop a dense page early; the eval's ordinary pages hold at most 21 entries. It goes on after each reply that was cut or added 40 cards or more; a front already read is left out, compared after the card key normalization, and a failed call is asked again once. A call starts only before 75 s of the request. A photo makes at most 10 such calls and 500 cards over its requests, a reply at most 100. When the request ends first, the answer carries the cards so far, `partial` `true` and a `cursor`; none is offered at a cap, or when the record did not keep it. The PM's probe of 2026-11-01 on the owner's page of 100 entries ran into the cap after about 51 s, at about 320 tokens a second.
- The schema asks for `kind`, `type`, `front`, `answer`, `topic`, `senses`, `extras`, `pronunciation`, `examples`, `table` and `cloze` on every card, and for no other field. Strict structured outputs require every key: `kind` and `type` are enums, a vocabulary, cloze or table card's `answer` and a note's `pronunciation` are `null`, a note's `senses` and `extras` and absent examples are empty lists, a sense without a label or an example has empty `labels` or `examples`, another label's `category` is `null`, `table` and `cloze` are `null` on every card but their own type, and the parser turns an absent value into an absent field. A table's row and column are integers the schema does not bound; the parser checks them.
- The prompt is `prompt_v8.ts` ([ADR 0076](adr/0076-ask-prompt-v8-for-each-sense-s-printed-labels-and-examples-transcribed-never-inferred-and-eval-labels-on-pages-that-print-none.md)): prompt v7 ([ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)) with each sense's printed labels, transcribed and never inferred, and its own examples, and grammar printed for the entry as a whole in `extras`. Prompt v7 is prompt v6 ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md)), itself prompt v5 ([ADR 0058](adr/0058-stop-asking-the-model-for-a-short-form-and-a-step-with-prompt-v5-and-add-a-notes-sample-with-the-writer-s-additions.md)) with atomic, exam-ready cards and a `type` on each, with a whole table on one table card, a cloze of one or more blanks, exception cards that state what the exception does, and examples only from the page. The model's instructions are the prompt version the server serves the caller, saved and activated in the web admin area ([ADR 0073](adr/0073-keep-the-extraction-prompt-as-append-only-versions-in-the-database-served-by-an-active-and-an-admin-only-test-label-and-record-what-served-each-call.md), [`server.md`](server.md) §Prompt store): the active version, or for an admin the test version while one is set. Version 1, the seed, is `prompt_v8.ts`'s `SYSTEM_PROMPT`, and a prompt change reaches users when an admin activates it, not on merge. The newest prompt file is `prompt_v10.ts` ([ADR 0124](adr/0124-send-a-fixed-output-contract-after-every-prompt-version-and-keep-a-reply-s-valid-cards.md)): the owner's store version 3, which assumes no subject, format or card type, tuned under the owner's direction of 2026-10-29 to put a vocabulary card's word on the front and its meaning on the back, to express a new question form as the nearest supported card type, and to leave the output's form, limits and check to the output contract. Under the direction of 2026-11-01 it is written for clear material ([ADR 0128](adr/0128-read-clear-material-in-photo-import-and-send-complex-material-to-the-external-ai-integration.md)): it analyses the material to find the words, terms or facts it teaches, keeps a list's numbering, ordinals and bullets off a card's front, and has none of version 3's lines for handwriting and complex notes. It is the text the eval measures by default. Before it, `prompt_v9.ts` ([ADR 0080](adr/0080-ask-prompt-v9-to-name-the-rule-on-every-card-under-it-and-save-it-as-a-version-an-admin-activates-after-the-pm-s-two-eval-runs.md)): prompt v8 with the rule's name on every card under a named rule. After the PM's two eval runs, migration `20261019230003_activate_prompt_v9.sql` saved it as version 2 and made it the active version, so it is what users are served; activating version 1 in the admin area serves prompt v8 again (ADR 0080's note). The owner saved version 3 and made it the active version on 2026-10-28 (#493). After the PM's run of v9 and v10 under the contract, the served version is store version 4, whose text is prompt v9's; v10 is not activated, since it was less faithful than v9 (ADR 0124's measured result). The contract, partial acceptance and the timeout apply to the served version. The schema, the parser, the output contract and the user turn with its language hints stay in the function.
- The model's instructions are the served version's text, unchanged, then a blank line and the output contract ([ADR 0124](adr/0124-send-a-fixed-output-contract-after-every-prompt-version-and-keep-a-reply-s-valid-cards.md)): `OUTPUT_CONTRACT` in `cards.ts`, beside the schema it describes, naming each field's meaning and every rule the parser checks, one sentence a rule, with the card limit and the reply's shape. It speaks of the material, never of a photo or a page, so another source can send it unchanged. Each refusal of the parser names its rule, and the rules are the contract's keys, so a check the contract does not state fails the type check.
- Prompt v9 asks, for notes, that cards hold the theory and the exceptions, each card one fact, small enough to recall and grade at once:
  - one card per fact, never a second card asking it again in another form; an answer that would list more than 5 items is split into several cards;
  - every definition and every rule on its own card, its front a short keyword naming the concept, such as "광합성" or "ㄷ 불규칙 (듣다)", never a question, and its answer the theory;
  - a list line, a group's name followed by its members, as a cloze card: its front the whole line with each member written `___` and its `cloze` the members in order, never a concept card answering the list; a line of more than 5 members as several cloze cards of at most 5 blanks, each keeping the group's name;
  - a cloze also for a sentence recalled in context that has no concept card of its own: one card with a blank for each word to recall, never asking what a concept or table card answers;
  - every line under a point, a member, case, condition or variant of its rule, covered by a card of its own or a cloze blank, never dropped or left only as an example, its card naming the rule it belongs to;
  - from prompt v9, every card made from the cases under a named rule, a concept, cloze or identify card alike, holding the rule's name in its front or answer, such as the cloze front "축약: ㅗ+ㅏ -> ___", never "ㅗ+ㅏ -> ___" alone (#261);
  - a table whose cells are to be memorised, such as a classification grid, as one table card: its front the table's name, its headers kept and the cells to recall as its blanks, never one card per cell or per row;
  - a judgment card for a rule that tells A from B, its front "A vs B" and its answer the test;
  - every tip, trap or note the writer added by hand, and every line marked as important, on its own card whose front starts with "★ ";
  - each exception on its own card, its answer stating what the exception does, as the page prints it, never only that it is an exception (#188);
  - a worked example never a concept, cloze or table card: it is an example of the card whose rule it illustrates, and only when the page prints it;
  - an example copied whole and character for character, only when the page prints it for that card, never shortened, completed, extended, translated or made up, left out when it cannot be read with confidence, and none when the model cannot see one printed for the card;
  - identify cards from the example sentences the page prints for a rule that sorts words into categories, at most 3 a rule, from printed sentences only;
  - no separate rules merged, no rule or exception left out as minor, and at most 100 cards, a page over the cap keeping its definitions, rules, judgment cards, handwritten tips and marked exceptions before its table and cloze cards, and those before its identify cards.
- Nothing is invented: the prompt asks for vocabulary exactly as written; for notes it lets the model name a concept and join or shorten the page's words, and forbids any fact, meaning, label or example the page does not show. The eval measures how often a model invents anyway.
- The reply is parsed, not trusted ([ADR 0124](adr/0124-send-a-fixed-output-contract-after-every-prompt-version-and-keep-a-reply-s-valid-cards.md)): anything other than JSON of exactly `{cards}`, a list of at most 100, is invalid output. Each card is then read alone, after trimming each field, and dropped when it breaks a rule of the contract, the reply's other cards being kept — a field empty once trimmed or over its limit, a topic over 60 characters, a key outside the schema such as v4's `short` or `step`, an unknown kind, a missing or unknown type, more than 5 examples, 10 senses or 10 extras, or a sense with more than 5 labels or 5 examples, a label of an unknown kind, a part of speech whose category is not on the list, or another label with a category, the old `{pos, meaning}` sense included — and so is a vocabulary card with an answer, no sense, a table or a cloze, or of type `table` or `cloze`; a note with senses, extras or a pronunciation; a concept or identify note without an answer or with a table or a cloze; a table card without its table, with an answer or a cloze; and a cloze card without its cloze, with an answer or a table. A table is invalid with no column or row, more than 8 columns or 20 rows, a row not as wide as the columns, no blank, or a blank outside the table, on an empty cell or given twice; a cloze is invalid with no answer, more than 10, or a number of answers other than its front's `___` blanks. A reply whose every card is dropped is invalid output too, since the photo saves nothing, and `no_cards` would say the page held none.
- A call that returns invalid output, fails over HTTP or the network, or has not answered, its body included, within 60 s (`MODEL_TIMEOUT_MS`, [ADR 0124](adr/0124-send-a-fixed-output-contract-after-every-prompt-version-and-keep-a-reply-s-valid-cards.md)), which aborts its request, fails. Its timeout is about four times the 2026-10-28 p90 of 15.6 s, under the Edge Function's 150 s request idle timeout (`supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/limits`, read 2026-10-30) and the web's 145 s wait for the function (`web/src/features/decks/read-photo.ts`, [`web-decks.md`](web-decks.md)). On the PM's eval run of #225's head, every first call of Claude Haiku 5.5 over the 55 samples returned valid output.
- **One retry** ([ADR 0130](adr/0130-retry-a-failed-photo-once-within-one-usage-and-record-each-extraction-s-outcome.md)): a first call that fails is made once more, with the same model, prompt and image, in the same request, the photo counting once. A valid empty list and a reply with kept cards are not retried.
  - The retry starts only while fewer than 75 s of the request have passed (`RETRY_START_LIMIT_MS` in `handler.ts`), so one that times out ends by about 135 s, under the Edge Function's request idle timeout of 150 s and its free plan's wall clock of 150 s (`supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/limits`, read 2026-11-01).
  - The answer is the last call's; a photo failed twice, or failed once past the limit, is `502`.
  - The retry is the handler's, not `extractCards`'s, so the eval still measures one call per image.
- A valid empty list is `422`.

### Switching the model

- The model is the caller's plan's, read from the database at each call, and an admin sets each plan's model in the web admin area ([ADR 0071](adr/0071-give-each-user-a-plan-whose-model-and-daily-limit-an-admin-sets-in-the-database-and-make-one-model-call-per-photo.md), [`server.md`](server.md) §Plans, [`web-admin.md`](web-admin.md)). No secret names a model: `CARD_MODEL` and `CARD_RETRY_MODEL` are no longer read, and the PM removes both after this change deploys.
- The plans are Free, the default, Pro and Max, which replaced Special. A user without a grant is on Free, and so is one whose Max has ended. The model is the one the user's plan version names; migration `20261026005136_plan_versions.sql` kept each plan's model and gave Max Special's ([`server.md`](server.md) §Plans, [ADR 0097](adr/0097-give-each-plan-versions-of-its-terms-with-a-start-free-10-a-month-pro-100-a-day-and-max-unlimited-to-1000-a-day-each-grant-taking-the-latest-at-its-monthly-renewal.md)).
- Earlier: on 2026-10-18 the owner first chose Claude Sonnet 5.5 from the notes report (issue #184), then moved back to Claude Haiku 5.5 the same day on a run over the 24 vocabulary pages, Haiku F1 99.0 at $0.0009 per image against Sonnet's 100.0 at $0.0161 (`docs/eval/extract-cards.md`).
- `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is a Supabase secret. Locally it is in `supabase/functions/.env`, which git ignores; creating that file is an owner action.

### Privacy

- The image is sent to OpenRouter and nowhere else: it is not stored, not written to disk, and not logged.
- Each request logs one JSON line with exactly `user_id`, `plan` (the caller's, or null when it was not read), `model` (the call's), `prompt_version` (the id of the version the call was made with, or null when none was made), `latency_ms`, `prompt_tokens`, `completion_tokens`, `dropped_cards` (the last reply's cards dropped for breaking a rule, or null when no reply was read as the schema's), `outcome_recorded` (whether the counted photo's outcome was recorded, or null when no photo was counted), and `outcome` (the response code, or `ok`). The tokens are the sum of the request's calls.
- Nothing else is printed: not the cards, the token, or a configuration value. An unexpected failure logs its line with outcome `internal` and propagates to the runtime, which answers `500`.

### Extraction limit

- Each plan version's limit of extractions per user, counted in `public.extraction_usage` over its period in Asia/Seoul ([`server.md`](server.md) §Plans, [ADR 0097](adr/0097-give-each-plan-versions-of-its-terms-with-a-start-free-10-a-month-pro-100-a-day-and-max-unlimited-to-1000-a-day-each-grant-taking-the-latest-at-its-monthly-renewal.md)):
  - Free, 10 a calendar month, from the 1st at 00:00;
  - Pro, 100 a day;
  - Max, unlimited with a fair-use cap of 1,000 a day.
- A photo counts when its call is about to start: the count is taken before the model is called, and a photo never sent is never counted.
- The function calls `public.begin_extraction` with the service-role key, before calling the model: it resolves the caller's plan version and prompt and returns the model, the limit, its period and the prompt version, and records the extraction when it is under the limit, with the plan, model and version that serve it, so a failed or empty extraction still counts ([ADR 0073](adr/0073-keep-the-extraction-prompt-as-append-only-versions-in-the-database-served-by-an-active-and-an-admin-only-test-label-and-record-what-served-each-call.md)). It returns the usage row it recorded, and a retry or a request with a cursor records nothing more: `public.resume_extraction` takes the cursor instead, deleting it, and returns that usage row with the model, prompt version and counts of its first read.
- **The outcome** ([ADR 0130](adr/0130-retry-a-failed-photo-once-within-one-usage-and-record-each-extraction-s-outcome.md)): before answering a counted photo, the function calls `public.finish_extraction` with that usage row, and it records on the photo's call its outcome (`ok`, `no_cards`, `invalid_output` or `model_error`), its calls (1 or 2), the request's latency, the last reply's cards answered and dropped, and each failed call's kind (`invalid_output`, `model_error` or `timeout`), with no image, card or prompt text ([`server.md`](server.md) §Prompt store). It also records `truncated`: for a photo answered with cards, whether its page was left unread past them, and otherwise whether the last reply was cut at the output cap ([ADR 0135](adr/0135-keep-the-cards-a-reply-cut-at-the-output-cap-completed-say-the-photo-was-read-in-part-and-measure-three-ways-to-read-a-dense-page-whole.md), [ADR 0138](adr/0138-read-a-photo-to-the-end-of-its-page-by-reading-a-cut-reply-on-in-the-request-and-going-on-with-a-cursor-the-photo-counted-once.md)). It records `continuations`, the calls that read on, and the cursor it offers. A request with a cursor adds its calls, its cards and one to `resumes` through `public.record_resumed_extraction`. The record is telemetry: when it fails, the photo is answered as it would have been, and the log line's `outcome_recorded` is false.
- The count and its insert run under a per-user advisory lock, so two concurrent requests cannot both take the last extraction. Migration `20261019230001_drop_old_function_entry_points.sql` dropped `public.consume_extraction` and `public.start_extraction`, which only a function deployed before plans or the prompt store called (ADR 0078).
- `public.extraction_allowance()` gives the app the caller's limit, its period and the photos counted in it, so it can say before reading how many photos the period leaves; it resolves the version and counts the period as `begin_extraction` does ([`server.md`](server.md), [ADR 0089](adr/0089-import-up-to-ten-photos-into-one-list-read-two-at-a-time-and-state-today-s-limit-before-reading.md)). The function's count stays the limit.

- Vocabulary pages: scores are per image, then averaged. A candidate card matches when its front and its senses' meanings, joined by "; ", or a note's `answer`, equal the ground truth's after the card key normalization ([`deck.md`](deck.md)); an image with no cards scores 0. A truth card holds its `senses`, read through the function's own sense parser, and the `examples` and `extras` its page prints for the entry as a whole (ADR 0076).
- Senses, over every candidate whose front matches an entry's after the same normalization, pooled over the vocabulary images: sense precision is the share of returned senses equal to one of the entry's in both labels and meaning, and sense recall the share of the entry's senses so returned ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md)).
- Over the matched cards of every vocabulary image, whose senses pair with the page's in order:
  - label precision is the share of returned labels whose text equals one the page prints for that sense, and label recall the share of printed labels so returned, after the same normalization;
  - category accuracy is the share of those equal labels whose kind and category are the page's too;
  - unprinted labels is the share of the senses the page prints no label for that were given one, which must be 0 (#228);
  - examples precision is the share of returned examples, on the card or a sense, that the page prints for the entry, and sense examples the share of examples printed under a sense that the returned sense holds;
  - extras is the share of printed extras returned with an equal label and text;
  - the invented rate is the share of returned fields — a sense's labels, a card's or a sense's examples, or a card's extras — where the page prints no such field;
  - each is shown as a dash when the configuration returned no such field, or the pages print none.
- Numbered fronts is, over every card returned for the vocabulary pages, the share whose front holds a list's number: a numeral alone, or one opening the front before "." or ")" or in brackets, such as "1. abandon". It must be 0 ([ADR 0128](adr/0128-read-clear-material-in-photo-import-and-send-complex-material-to-the-external-ai-integration.md)). Such a card also matches no entry, so it lowers F1.
- The report gives these per vocabulary page too, with its F1, so a page written for one measure reads on its own (ADR 0076).
- Notes pages: a page's facts are its rules and exceptions, each holding the worked examples the page prints for it; a worked example is not a fact. A card covers a fact when its front and answer hold every one of the fact's terms after the same normalization; its examples do not count, since another rule's worked example can hold a rule's terms ([ADR 0055](adr/0055-cover-everything-a-notes-page-teaches-at-step-5-with-prompt-v4-and-eval-a-dense-page-by-coverage-per-page.md)).
  - Coverage is the share of the page's facts that some card covers, per image and then averaged. It equals coverage at step 5 in reports before prompt v5, whose cards carry no step of their own; the facts keep their `step`, which no metric reads ([ADR 0058](adr/0058-stop-asking-the-model-for-a-short-form-and-a-step-with-prompt-v5-and-add-a-notes-sample-with-the-writer-s-additions.md)).
  - Unsupported cards are the share of returned cards that cover no fact, and invented examples the share of returned examples the page does not print. A returned example counts as printed when it and one of the page's worked examples contain each other, or when a line the page prints contains it, as a rule's own worked example sits in the rule's line.
  - Long answers are the share of returned cards whose answer lists more than 5 items, counting its parts between commas, middle dots, slashes, semicolons and line breaks: the cards prompt v6's atomic rule splits ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md)). A table card is not counted, since its answer is its whole table.
  - Bare exceptions are the share of returned cards whose answer names an exception, "예외" or "exception", yet covers no fact: an exception card that does not state what the exception does (#188, [ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)).
  - Table blanks are, over the pages whose truth names `tableBlanks`, the share of those cells that one blank cell of a table card holds, every one of the blank's terms after the same normalization. Cloze blanks are the same over `clozeBlanks` and one answer of a cloze card. Each is a dash where no page names any.
  - Term matching undercounts a fact the model paraphrased past its terms, in coverage and as an unsupported card: the cost of scoring with no judge model.

#### Photo

- The camera and the library are `image_picker`'s system camera and its multi-image picker with a limit, each asked for at most 2048 px per side, without full metadata so the library needs no photo-library permission. The settings page is iOS's `app-settings:` address, opened through `url_launcher`.
- Every photo is decoded, turned upright by its EXIF orientation, scaled so its long side is at most 2048 px keeping its aspect ratio, and encoded as JPEG at quality 85, stepping down to 40 until it is at most 6 MiB. This runs on a background isolate with the pure-Dart `image` package.
- A file `image` cannot decode, such as a HEIC photo, is `PhotoUnreadable`.

#### Request and failures

- `HttpExtractionClient` posts `{"image": <base64 JPEG>}`, with `"cursor"` when reading a page on, to `<SUPABASE_URL>/functions/v1/extract-cards` with the session's access token as `Authorization: Bearer`, through `package:http` with an abortable request. Without a session it sends nothing.
- The answer is parsed at the boundary: a `200` must hold a non-empty `cards` list, or for a request with a cursor any list, each card a non-empty `front`, `answer` and `topic` of at most 60 characters, a `kind` of `vocabulary` or `note`, and a list of non-empty `examples` where present. No other field stands in for one of these (ADR 0078). A vocabulary card's `senses` are each `{labels, meaning, examples}`, its labels `{kind, category, text}`, as `deck`'s `decodeSense` reads them ([ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md)); any other sense shape, the function's `{pos, meaning}` before #228's PR 1 included, is `ServiceFailed`.
  - Beside the cards it reads `cursor`, a non-empty string, only where `partial` is `true`, and no other field.
- Each failure is a `PhotoImportFailure`, shown in place of the screen with its ARB message. Its top bar's close is its one way out; the bottom bar holds what the table offers besides, and is absent where it offers nothing else ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-start-a-deck-or-a-card-from-one-sheet-that-holds-the-photo-source-and-name-public-decks-once.md)). The retake takes another photo from the photo's source, named as on the list:

| Failure | When | Offers |
|---|---|---|
| `ExtractionOffline` | the request could not reach the server | retry, retake |
| `PhotoRefused` | `413` or `415` | retake |
| `NoCardsFound` | `422` | retake |
| `PhotoLimitReached` | `429`; its message states the plan's limit, the body's `photo_limit`, for its `period`: "오늘 사진으로 만들 수 있는 {limit}장을 다 썼어요. 내일 다시 시도해 주세요." a day, "이번 달 사진으로 만들 수 있는 {limit}장을 다 썼어요. 다음 달 1일에 다시 시도해 주세요." a month; a `429` without both, as a gateway might send, is a `ServiceFailed` | the top bar's close |
| `ModelFailed` | `502` | retry, retake |
| `SignInRequired` | `401`, or no session | the top bar's close |
| `AccountSuspendedFailure` | `403` with the error `suspended`; its message says the account is suspended and where to write | the top bar's close |

- A `DeckFailure` while reading the deck or saving is shown in a snack bar, as the deck screens show one; `DeckDeleted` says the deck was deleted meanwhile.

- `app/ios/Runner/Info.plist` holds `NSCameraUsageDescription` and `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` in English, and `app/ios/Runner/ko.lproj/InfoPlist.strings` and `en.lproj/InfoPlist.strings` hold them in Korean and English.
