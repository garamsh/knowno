# ADR 0244: Judge a reported note once by a model after two reporters, within a daily spend cap, ship it judge-only, and let it hide or dismiss only by an admin's switch with every action undoable

> Status: accepted; its Vault secret `triage_function_url` superseded by ADR 0245
> Date: 2026-11-10

Append-only: once merged, the body below is not rewritten. A fact later found wrong is corrected in an appended note, not edited out; a revised decision is a new ADR that supersedes this one.

## Context

- **Issue #887**, part of #591 and #590. [ADR 0240](0240-keep-note-screening-to-rules-and-let-reports-catch-the-rest-and-return-each-finding-s-field.md) kept no model at publish and left what the rules miss to reports, three of which hide a note until an admin decides. The owner asked on 2026-11-10 for a model to judge reported notes, so the admins' queue is shorter, at a cost the owner sets.
- **Cost rules binding the design** (#590's comment of 2026-11-03): one verdict per note by its content hash; a judgment only from two distinct reporters; a cheap model, a stronger one only when unsure; a bounded payload; scheduled batches, severe categories at once; a daily spend cap past which no model is called and everything goes to a person; a per-user daily report cap; a lower weight for often-dismissed reporters.
- **What reports carry.** `deck_reports` holds a reporter, a deck and one of five reasons, no text (the fifth, `sexual`, is added by this change so that severe has a reason of its own). A report cannot name the card it is about.

## Decision

- **A server job judges reported notes.** `triage-reports`, an Edge Function with the same shape as `publish-note`, runs on a schedule and, for the one severe case below, at once. Nothing is judged at publish; ADR 0240 stands.
- **One verdict per content.** `private.triage_verdicts` is keyed by the note's content hash, `private.note_content_hash`. A verdict is reused until a card or the text changes, then the changed note is due again if it is still reported by two.
- **Due means reporters of weight 2, no verdict for the current hash, a live public note,** and spend, the day's notes and the batch under their caps. A reporter weighs 1, or 0.5 when they made at least 5 reports in the last 90 days and more than half were dismissed; two ordinary reporters make 2, and reporters who together weigh under 2 leave the note to people. The first reporter triggers nothing.
- **Severe goes at once.** A note with a `sexual` report among two reporters is judged by the trigger on `deck_reports` as the second reporter arrives; every other reason, `inappropriate` included, waits for the batch, run every 5 minutes. The trigger swallows any error of its own, so a report is never refused for a failing worker call. A verdict whose category is `severe` is never acted on.
- **Cheap first.** A configuration names a cheap and a strong model, each with its provider. The strong one is called only when the cheap one's confidence is below the configuration's `escalate_below`, or its category is `other`.
- **A bounded payload,** built by the function: the name and description; the cards the rules flagged; a fixed sample of the others, ordered by the hash of the content hash and the card hash, so a retry sends the same cards; all under the configuration's token cap. Report reasons go in the prompt. Cards matching the report's reason are met through the rules' categories: a reason maps to the categories that explain it (`spam` to phone, link, kakao_id, contact and repeat; `inappropriate` and `sexual` to term; `copyright` and `other` to none), and under the token cap the name and description come first, then flagged cards of those categories, then other flagged cards, then the sample. Every string is sent as a JSON-encoded line inside `<note>` with `<` and `>` escaped, so a card cannot close the block or add an instruction; the budget counts the encoded bytes.
- **A verdict** holds a category (`spam`, `ad`, `inappropriate`, `copyright`, `severe`, `other`, `ok`, or `unjudged`), a confidence, the quoted passage and its field, the card's hash, the tier, provider, model and configuration, tokens, and the cost. A reply that does not parse after tokens were spent is recorded `unjudged`, goes to a person and is not retried; a network failure, a 429 or a 5xx records nothing, releases the note with a failure and leaves it due after a wait (below).
- **The daily spend cap is `private.triage_settings.daily_cap_usd`,** default 0.50, counted over the UTC day's rows of `private.triage_spend`, a ledger the function writes after every provider call through `record_triage_spend`, whether the call produced a verdict or not; a verdict's own cost is never the source. A reply that omits its usage, and a call that may have run without telling (unreachable, timed out), is charged its ceiling, every request byte a token in plus the answer cap out, marked estimated; a refusal with an error status costs nothing. `record_triage_spend` answers whether the cap is still open, and the function skips the strong call when it is not. At or past the cap `triage_due` returns no note and `triage_claim` none, so everything stays in the human queue. Notes judged in parallel can overshoot the cap by what is in flight, at most the batch's ceilings, since each checks the cap only as it is charged. The admin's two check calls when saving a configuration are not in the ledger. `admin_resume_triage` stamps `resumed_at`, and the undo window counts only from it, so a resume is not undone at once by the actions that paused it.
- **Configuration follows ADRs 0162 and 0182:** `private.triage_configurations` is append-only, labelled `active` and `test`; a save is a settings route of the function that checks both tiers with one call each, parsed, through the key already in Vault, and then a service-only function; an admin activates it, refused while a provider has no key. The prompt text is held in the configuration, not in the prompt store, since a verdict names its configuration and so the prompt it was judged by. ADR 0162's trial on real photos is replaced by judge-only mode, which is the trial: verdicts accrue beside the admins' own decisions and `admin_triage_agreement` compares them. No configuration is seeded, so the job does nothing until an admin saves and activates one.
- **Modes.** `private.triage_settings.mode` is `judge_only`, the default, or `acting`, set by an admin. Judge-only records verdicts and changes no note, report or audit row beyond the verdict. Acting, while not paused, may only:
  - hide a note judged `spam` or `ad` at or above `act_threshold` (the note becomes `held` with the `ai` hold, as a report hold does);
  - dismiss the reports on a note judged `ok` at or above the threshold, after archiving them.
  It never unpublishes, removes or suspends, never acts on `copyright`, `severe`, `other` or `inappropriate`, and never on `unjudged`; copyright and `sexual` always go to a person.
- **Accountability.** Every action writes `private.triage_actions` and an audit row with no admin id and `admin_email = 'ai'`, naming the model and the reason. `admin_undo_triage_action` reverses one (unhides the note, or restores the archived reports). A fixed share of actions, `sample_percent` (10), chosen by a hash of the content hash, is queued for a person to re-check. Automation pauses itself, setting `paused_at`, when over the window `undo_window_hours` at least `undo_min_actions` actions stand and the share undone passes `undo_threshold`; an admin resumes it.
- **Agreement.** `admin_triage_agreement` compares each verdict with the first decision an admin made on the same note after it: a verdict that the note is bad agrees with hide, unpublish and remove; a verdict `ok` agrees with dismiss, allow and unhide.
- **A claim is a lease.** `private.triage_attempts` holds one row per note: `claim` takes it only when it is free or older than `lease_minutes` (10), by one atomic upsert, so two overlapping sweeps never judge a note twice. A failed note waits `retry_minutes` (15) doubling with each failure and is left to people after `max_failures` (3); a note whose content hash changed starts again. A missing key releases the note without a failure.
- **Acting guards.** Acting refuses to hide a note holding any hold, so it never overwrites a report hold or a person's decision, and refuses to dismiss while a `copyright` or `sexual` report exists. Undoing a hide whose `ai` hold is gone answers `superseded` and changes nothing; resolving an appeal answers `restored`, `not_restored` or `upheld`. An undo restores reports as they were, without the report cap and dismissal marks.
- **Report cap and weight.** `private.report_log` records every report, kept after dismissal and deck deletion; a before-insert trigger on `deck_reports` refuses a report past `reporter_daily_cap` (20) a day with error 53400. A report deleted while its note stands is a dismissal and counts against the reporter's weight; a cascade from a deleted deck does not.
- **Provider keys.** A key an active triage configuration names is in use, so `admin_remove_provider_key` refuses it and `admin_provider_keys` says so.
- **Appeals.** The owner of a note an action hid may appeal once per standing action; the appeal reaches the admin queue, and only an admin resolves it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Judging at the first report.** It spends on every spiteful report; two distinct users is #590's rule.
- **A model at publish.** ADR 0240 decided against it.
- **A separate prompt table.** A verdict would need two ids to name what judged it; the configuration holds the prompt.
- **Acting from the start.** The owner's cost rules and the accountability the issue asks for are both met only by measuring first; judge-only is the default and the agreement RPC is the measure.
- **Counting severity from the model's reply to decide timing.** The model is not called until the note is judged, so timing must come from the report's reason.

## Consequences

- **Cost is bounded per day,** by the cap, and per note by the payload cap; the PR states the expected monthly cost at three volumes.
- **A note over the cap waits for a person or the next UTC day.**
- **Web (#888) and app (#889)** read the functions §Report triage of [`server.md`](../server.md) lists.
- **The owner creates a Vault secret** `triage_function_url`, as for `screening_function_url`; without it the sweeper and the trigger do nothing.
