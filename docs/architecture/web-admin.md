# Web admin

The web's `admin` feature: the admin area at `/admin`, its gate, sections, actions and reads. The role, the audit log and the functions behind it are [`server.md`](server.md)'s.

## Current decisions

### Area

- Operators run the service from `/admin`, in the `(shell)` route group, on the web only; the app has no admin screen ([ADR 0070](adr/0070-run-the-service-from-a-web-admin-area-whose-role-and-audit-log-live-in-the-database-and-whose-every-read-and-write-is-checked-there.md)). Its layout, `web/src/app/(shell)/admin/layout.tsx`, renders nothing until the server's `is_admin` answers, inside a Suspense boundary with an empty fallback: an admin then sees the section menu and the page; anyone else gets the not-found page, streamed as a deck that does not exist is; a server that cannot answer gets the error page. The area's pages set no metadata, so no title names it, and no link elsewhere leads to it.
- The role and every check live in the database ([`server.md`](server.md) §Admin area): every read and write is one of the server's admin functions, called with the user's session and the publishable key, and each refuses a caller who is not an admin. The web holds no service-role key and no admin's email or id.
- Each section's page is headed by its name in the menu (#326).
- Every page follows [`design.md`](design.md) §Copy and §Details, as the web's settings and deck pages do (#509): each list or set of facts is one group of hairline rows on the `group` radius under a muted section label, through `admin-section.tsx`'s `AdminSection` and its `group`, `row`, `button` and `field` classes; a state is the one chip (`web/src/design/chip.tsx`), 숨김, 신고 n, 정지됨 and 공유 삭제 in amber, 사용 중, 관리자, 무제한 and the user's plan in the brand tint, the rest neutral, and no state is red, which means 모른다, but a failure rate over 5%, drawn in `dont-know-text` ([ADR 0155](adr/0155-organise-the-admin-area-in-five-sections-by-the-job-to-do-and-show-only-what-an-operator-acts-on.md)); a card of 처리할 일 or 사진 AI and a view switch are solid surfaces on their edge, and a button in a card or a row is outlined, a tap target high, in the `label` type (`admin-section.tsx`'s `card` and `button.row`); a field inside a group has no border until it is focused, then a 1.5 px ink outline; a page has at most one primary (지정, 저장), the plans page none, since it draws a form per plan, and every other button is outlined; a removed line of the prompt diff is on the amber tint; the one action that cannot be undone, 공유에서 영구 삭제, opens from red text at the end of the deck's page and under a note row's ⋯; and the user's 이용 정지 is the user page's last section. The gate sets `word-break: keep-all` over every page, so Korean breaks only between words. A page draws its title, the body's 16 and the caption's 13, beside a card's figure; an empty list says what is true, then what to do; and a page that could not read says what failed and what to do, with "다시 시도" unless the reader is not an admin.
- The section menu, "관리 메뉴", a navigation landmark with the current section marked `aria-current="page"` in `accent` on the brand tint: 처리할 일, 노트, 사용자, 사진 AI and 설정, one per job ([ADR 0155](adr/0155-organise-the-admin-area-in-five-sections-by-the-job-to-do-and-show-only-what-an-operator-acts-on.md)). 처리할 일 carries the open reports' count, read beside the role check in the gate; a count that cannot be read shows none, and 처리할 일 says what failed.
  - An **open report** is a reported note others can still see: public, not hidden and not deleted, read through `admin_note_list('reported')`. 숨기기 and 문제없음 each take a note off it; a note the trigger hid at three reports is under 숨긴 노트 with its count.
  - **처리할 일** (`/admin`): three cards, each with one action, and a place for a later queue's card:
    - 새 신고, the open reports and the oldest one's age, with 신고 보기;
    - 사진 읽기 실패율 · 7일, the share of the last 7 UTC days' recorded calls that ended `invalid_output` or `model_error`, from `admin_extraction_failures(7)`, in `dont-know-text` over 5%, with 자세히;
    - 테스트 중인 프롬프트, the version under test and its day under test from `admin_prompt_labels`, with 적용 또는 끝내기 to its page, or 없음 with 사진 AI 보기;
    - then "이번 주", the last 7 days against the 7 before: new users and the daily average of users who studied, by study days from 04:00 in the cookie's zone (`admin_week_activity`), and the photos read and their estimated cost by UTC days (`admin_extraction_breakdown(14)`), each with its change as a neutral chip unless the week before was 0. Each photo is costed at its model's measured cost from `web/src/features/admin/model-costs.ts`, a photo counted before calls were recorded (기록 전) at the model of Free's current version, and one a never-measured model served is counted apart and named.
  - **노트** (`/admin/decks`): the public live notes in three views, each its own address under the switch "노트 보기": 신고된 노트 n, the open reports, the oldest first report first (the address alone); 모든 공개 노트 (`?view=public`), newest published first; and 숨긴 노트 (`?view=hidden`), the most reported first. One search, through the address's `q`, covers the note's name and its owner's email, and keeps the view. Each row shows the name with its 신고 n and 숨김 chips, the owner's email, cards, copies ("가져오기 n회") and reasons, and three actions fixed in place: 카드 보기, 문제없음 (신고 정리), disabled while the note has no report, and 숨기기, or 숨김 해제 on a hidden note; under ⋯, a native disclosure, 만든 사람 보기 and 공유에서 영구 삭제. `/admin/decks/[id]` opens any deck read-only, its cards' sides in two columns, with 숨기기 or 숨김 해제, 비공개로 전환 and 신고 정리, and is not found for an id that is no deck's ([ADR 0072](adr/0072-suspend-a-user-through-supabase-auth-s-ban-and-restrictive-write-policies-remove-a-public-deck-from-sharing-for-good-and-read-any-deck-in-the-admin-area.md)). A public deck not yet removed offers "공유에서 영구 삭제", which asks for a reason; a removed deck shows the reason and the day instead.
  - **사용자** (`/admin/users`): the search by email as the admin types, 250 ms after the last keystroke, starting from the address's `q`: the shown results stay until the next arrive, an answer to an older query is dropped, a `status` region reads the count out, and "더 보기" adds the next 20 ([ADR 0072](adr/0072-suspend-a-user-through-supabase-auth-s-ban-and-restrictive-write-policies-remove-a-public-deck-from-sharing-for-good-and-read-any-deck-in-the-admin-area.md)). The search lives in the address (#326): each search, and each page "더 보기" adds, replaces it with `?q=` and `&page=` through the browser's `history.replaceState`, which Next.js keeps its router in step with and which reads nothing again; an address with `q` opens with its pages through `page`, a whole number of up to three digits, read on the server, so going back from a user's page shows the same results. The address carries only the query; every read is still checked in the database. `/admin/users/[id]` holds everything about one user, its email headed by the plan, 무제한, 관리자 and 정지됨 chips:
    - "계정": the sign-up date, the last study, notes and cards, and the photos counted in the period of the user's plan version against its limit, or against "무제한" when no limit applies to the user, "이번 달 사진 추출" on a monthly plan and "오늘 사진 추출" otherwise, both in Asia/Seoul;
    - "요금제": the plan with its terms, such as "한 달 10장" or "무제한 (하루 1000장까지)", its model, the grant behind it with its granter and its last day, and the next renewal, the 1st at 00:00 Asia/Seoul, when the user takes the version then in effect; "요금제 바꾸기", a native disclosure, opens a form that puts the user on 프로, or on 맥스 until the end of a chosen day's study day in the admin's zone, or without an end, on the version a user subscribing now takes, and "무료로 되돌리기", asked first, which removes the grant;
    - "무제한" ([ADR 0157](adr/0157-let-admins-and-users-granted-unlimited-use-past-every-photo-limit-decided-in-the-server-s-metering-with-the-grant-beside-the-plan.md), [`server.md`](server.md) §Plans): whether no photo limit applies now, as `admin_user_unlimited` answers it, "무제한 · 관리자" for an admin, who needs no grant, "무제한" for a holder and "요금제 한도대로" otherwise; the grant with its granter and its last day, or "끝난 날" once it has passed; "무제한 바꾸기", a native disclosure, opens a form that grants 무제한 until the end of a chosen day's study day in the admin's zone, or without an end, replacing any grant, and "무제한 해제", asked first, which removes it and leaves the user on its plan;
    - "노트": every live note of the user, private ones too, each with its state and reports, each opening the note;
    - "이용 정지": "이용 정지" asks for a reason and suspends the user, and a suspended user shows "정지됨" with the reason, when and by whom, and "정지 해제". Nothing here edits or deletes an account or a deck.
  - **사진 AI** (`/admin/ai`): the extraction prompt's versions ([ADR 0073](adr/0073-keep-the-extraction-prompt-as-append-only-versions-in-the-database-served-by-an-active-and-an-admin-only-test-label-and-record-what-served-each-call.md), [`server.md`](server.md) §Prompt store) and the outcomes `extract-cards` records ([ADR 0130](adr/0130-retry-a-failed-photo-once-within-one-usage-and-record-each-extraction-s-outcome.md), [`ai-extraction.md`](ai-extraction.md)), read through `admin_extraction_failures(30)`:
    - three cards: 사용 중 프롬프트, the active version and its failure rate over 7 UTC days, with 새 버전 쓰기; 테스트 중, the test version, its rate and calls over 30 days, with 모두에게 적용, or 없음; and "사진 한 장으로 시험", which names the version it runs, the test version while one is set and the active one otherwise. The trial runs `extract-cards` on one photo as the admin and lists the cards, or says why there are none; no limit applies to an admin ([ADR 0157](adr/0157-let-admins-and-users-granted-unlimited-use-past-every-photo-limit-decided-in-the-server-s-metering-with-the-grant-beside-the-plan.md)), and the photo is still recorded. The browser first turns the photo upright and scales it as the app does, a JPEG with its long side at most 2048 px, stepping its quality down until it is at most 900,000 bytes, under a Server Action's default 1 MB body;
    - "버전별 결과": every saved version, newest first, with its labels 사용 중 and 테스트, note, its 30 days' calls, 카드 없음, 형식 오류 and 모델 오류, and its failure rate, in `dont-know-text` over 5%, or "기록 없음", each opening the version.
    - `/admin/ai/new`, "새 버전": one line saying the function's fixed output contract, the cards' fields and rules, is sent after every version ([ADR 0124](adr/0124-send-a-fixed-output-contract-after-every-prompt-version-and-keep-a-reply-s-valid-cards.md)), then a form that saves the text with a note as a new version, starting from the active version or from the one the address's `from` names, and opens it. Saving serves it to no one.
    - `/admin/ai/[id]` shows a version's note, length, time and author and its 30 days' counts; "모든 사용자에게 적용", "관리자만 테스트" while it is neither, and "테스트 끝내기" while it is the test version; "날짜별 실패율", each day's rate as a bar, as the 기록 page draws its weeks ([`web-study.md`](web-study.md)): today's in ink, the others ink at 55%, a day without a recorded call drawing none, over a scale from 0% to the highest day's rate rounded up to 5%, one image named by every day's rate; unless it is active, its lines against the active version, each changed line marked − or + and named "뺌" or "더함" for a screen reader; "최근 추출", its recorded calls from `admin_version_calls`, 50 at a time, newest first, each with its outcome as a chip (a failure in amber), time, plan, calls with each failed call's kind, latency, cards answered and cards dropped (거른 카드), "이전 추출 더 보기" opening the 50 before the last through the address's `before` and "최신 추출로" returning, the user a call served neither read nor shown; and its text, with "이 버전에서 새 버전 쓰기". An id that is no version's is not found.
  - **설정** (`/admin/settings`): three pages under the switch "설정 보기":
    - **요금제** (`/admin/settings`): a section per plan, 무료, 프로 and 맥스 ([ADR 0097](adr/0097-give-each-plan-versions-of-its-terms-with-a-start-free-10-a-month-pro-100-a-day-and-max-unlimited-to-1000-a-day-each-grant-taking-the-latest-at-its-monthly-renewal.md)): the terms a user subscribing now takes, as "지금 가입하면 한 달 10장 · model", with the eval's measured cost per photo beside a measured model; a form, "새 버전", starting from those terms: the quota (한 달에, 하루에, or 무제한 with its fair-use cap a day), the number of photos, the model, the start, 00:00 Asia/Seoul on the day chosen or now when it is empty, and whether existing users take it from its start rather than at their renewal on the 1st, which only a change in their favour may; and every version, the latest start first, with its terms, model, start, whether it applies to existing users and its author. Saving first reads OpenRouter's public model list, `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models`, the one request the web makes outside Supabase: a model must take image input and support structured outputs, the eval's rule, or it is not saved, and none is while the list cannot be read. The server checks the role, the terms, the start and the favour, and records the version ([ADR 0071](adr/0071-give-each-user-a-plan-whose-model-and-daily-limit-an-admin-sets-in-the-database-and-make-one-model-call-per-photo.md)).
    - **관리자** (`/admin/settings/admins`): the admins, earliest granted first, with who granted each and when, "해제" on every one but the signed-in admin's own, marked "(나)", and the form "관리자 지정" by an existing user's email.
    - **작업 기록** (`/admin/settings/audit`): the latest 100 admin writes, newest first, each its action, target (linked when it is a deck, a user, a plan or a prompt version), time and admin.
  - **The addresses before the five sections** redirect behind the gate, so a user who is not an admin still gets the not-found page: `/admin/reports` to `/admin/decks`; `/admin/plans`, `/admin/admins` and `/admin/audit` to their 설정 pages; `/admin/extractions` and `/admin/prompts` to `/admin/ai`, `/admin/prompts?from=` to `/admin/ai/new?from=`; and `/admin/prompts/[id]` to `/admin/ai/[id]`.
- Every action asks first: its button opens a group named by the question, with "취소" and, last, its confirm button, focused, in the primary fill; the one action that cannot be undone, 공유에서 영구 삭제, confirms with "영구 삭제" as a red final button, `dont-know-text` under `on-dont-know-text`, as `design.md` §Principles 5 draws an irreversible action's last step (#280); once confirmed, the Server Action in `admin`'s `actions.ts` calls the server, returns the sentence a `status` region shows, and revalidates `/admin`. The actions are 숨기기, 숨김 해제, 비공개로 전환, 신고 정리 (문제없음 on a 노트 row) and 공유에서 영구 삭제 on a deck, 무제한 해제, 이용 정지 and 정지 해제 on a user, 모든 사용자에게 적용, 관리자만 테스트 and 테스트 끝내기 on a prompt version, and 해제 on an admin; the prompt form and the trial submit as forms; the two that take a reason open a field for it first, and confirm only once it holds one; the plan's and 무제한's grant forms submit through `useActionState`. A refusal returns "관리자만 할 수 있어요." and changes nothing.
- The `admin` feature holds the reads (`read-admin.ts`), an extraction's outcome read whole or not at all, as the server records its parts together, the outcomes summed by day and by version in `outcomes.ts`, the two weeks' photos and cost in `usage.ts`, each row parsed at the boundary and a failure returned as a value, the Server Actions, one `<verb>-<noun>.ts` file each (`hide-deck.ts`, `unhide-deck.ts`, `unpublish-deck.ts`, `dismiss-reports.ts`, `grant-admin.ts`, `revoke-admin.ts`, `create-plan-version.ts`, `grant-plan.ts`, `revoke-plan.ts`, `grant-unlimited.ts`, `revoke-unlimited.ts`, `suspend-user.ts`, `unsuspend-user.ts`, `remove-deck.ts`, `save-prompt.ts`, `activate-prompt.ts`, `test-prompt.ts`, `clear-test-prompt.ts`, `try-prompt.ts`, and `find-users.ts` for the search as one types) over the shared call and refusal in `action-call.ts`, which reads a deck's id through `decks`' `parseDeckId` and a user's through its own `parseUserId`, refusing any other as malformed without asking the server, as the routes' pages answer not found, re-exported by `actions.ts`, and the components; the routes' `_components/content.tsx` composes them. Times show in the cookie's zone.
