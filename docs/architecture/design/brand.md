# Design brand

The brand on the phone and the web: the name, the marks and how they are generated, the mascot's expressions, motion and moments, where each mark appears, the icons and when one is drawn, and the launcher icons and splash.

## Current decisions

### Name and marks

- The app's name is 알아몰라 in Korean and knowno in English and as the brand ([ADR 0134](../adr/0134-name-the-app-knowno-serve-it-at-knowno-app-identify-it-as-app-knowno-and-reach-the-backend-at-api-knowno-app.md)).
- The marks are drawn around the coral mascot the owner chose ([ADR 0141](../adr/0141-polish-the-coral-mascot-make-coral-the-primary-action-and-place-its-expressions-on-both-clients.md)): a broad bean with big round eyes looking up, pink cheeks, little feet and one rounded arm to its chin, with no fingers. They are:
  - the mark alone, and the expressions;
  - the mark close up on cobalt: the launcher icon and the favicon ([ADR 0152](../adr/0152-let-the-mascot-blink-and-breathe-from-its-own-drawing-and-put-the-launcher-icon-on-cobalt.md));
  - the horizontal and stacked lockups: the mark on its circle beside or over one name, 알아몰라 for Korean and on the web and knowno for any other language on the phone, never both; on a white circle with its word cobalt `#2547D0` for a light ground, on a dark card `#232F4E` circle with its word `#8AB4FF` for a dark one ([ADR 0153](../adr/0153-draw-the-lockups-in-cobalt-on-a-white-or-dark-card-circle.md), [ADR 0154](../adr/0154-draw-the-logo-with-one-name-in-the-reader-s-language-korean-or-english.md)). Both names are drawn 446 across, so a stacked lockup is 446 by 421 with 알아몰라 and 446 by 390 with knowno, the horizontal one 674 by 200.
- Where they appear: the app's name in `title` heads 홈, the first run's included; the stacked lockup, `AppSize.stackedLockupWidth` wide, on the sign-in screen, in the phone's language; on the web the stacked lockup on `/login`, in 알아몰라, and the horizontal lockup atop the side menu, which the phone does not bundle.

### Generated files

- `tools/brand/` holds the sources: `body.svg` the one body; `mark.svg`, `happy.svg`, `sad.svg` and `sleepy.svg` the drawings over it, each moving part named by its class (`eye`, `pupil`, `arms`, `zz`); `coach-hand.svg` the study coach's pointing hand ([ADR 0166](../adr/0166-draw-no-mascot-during-a-study-session-let-it-present-the-coach-with-a-back-and-a-next-and-a-tilted-hand-and-fill-an-empty-day-s-bar.md)); and `wordmarks.json` the two names, outlined from Pretendard ExtraBold.
- `tools/brand/generate.mjs`, run by `make brand`, writes every brand file from them, and none is edited by hand: the SVGs in `web/public/brand/`, `web/public/favicon.svg`, the web's icons, `app/assets/brand/` (PNGs at 1x to 3x with the icon and splash sources), the mascot's moving parts with each eye's closed lid in `app/lib/design/mascot_drawings.dart` and `web/src/design/mascot-drawings.ts` ([ADR 0152](../adr/0152-let-the-mascot-blink-and-breathe-from-its-own-drawing-and-put-the-launcher-icon-on-cobalt.md)), and the coach hand's paths in `app/lib/study/src/coach_hand_drawing.dart` and `web/src/features/study/components/coach-hand-drawing.ts`, drawn by each client in its theme's tokens.
- `AppBrand` in `app/lib/design/theme.dart` names the app's files, the stacked lockup by locale: `knowno-stacked-ko-*.png` for Korean, `knowno-stacked-en-*.png` for any other. The web, in Korean alone, has the 알아몰라 lockups `knowno-lockup-*.svg` and `knowno-stacked-*.svg`; a lockup's `<title>` and alt text read the name it shows.
- The launcher icons are generated by `flutter_launcher_icons` from `app/assets/brand/icon/app-icon-light-1024.png`, the mark close up on cobalt `#2547D0`, with `app-icon-dark-1024.png`, the same on `#111A2E`, as iOS 18's dark icon, and `app-icon-foreground-1024.png`, the mark on no ground, as Android's adaptive foreground over cobalt ([ADR 0152](../adr/0152-let-the-mascot-blink-and-breathe-from-its-own-drawing-and-put-the-launcher-icon-on-cobalt.md)).
- The native splash is generated by `flutter_native_splash` from `app/assets/brand/splash/`, on the screens' own grounds: the mark alone on `#EEF3FF`, and the mark on a `#232F4E` circle on `#111A2E` in dark mode, Android 12's splash icon included.
- Both generators are pinned exactly in `app/pubspec.yaml`, which holds their configuration. After `make brand`, revert the two edits they make beyond their output: `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_GENERATE_SWIFT_ASSET_SYMBOL_EXTENSIONS` in `ios/Runner.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj` and the rewrite of `ios/Runner/Info.plist`.

### Mascot

- The mascot is `Mascot` in `app/lib/design/mascot.dart` and in `web/src/design/brand.tsx`, one of four `MascotExpression`s, on no ground of its own, hidden from screen readers, carrying no badge ([ADR 0141](../adr/0141-polish-the-coral-mascot-make-coral-the-primary-action-and-place-its-expressions-on-both-clients.md), [ADR 0116](../adr/0116-draw-an-icon-only-where-it-says-what-the-words-beside-it-do-not-or-follows-the-platform.md)).
- 홈's mascot is the way into the chat on both clients, a button on the phone and a link on the web, named "말랑이와 대화하기", with a speech bubble over it while cards are ready and, once a day, a line of the day's state ([ADR 0248](../adr/0248-meet-malangi-on-home-alone-offer-the-chat-once-a-day-in-a-bubble-over-the-mascot-and-open-it-naming-no-note.md), [`home.md`](../home.md), [`web-assistant.md`](../web-assistant.md)).

- It moves the same on both clients, within its own widget or component ([ADR 0152](../adr/0152-let-the-mascot-blink-and-breathe-from-its-own-drawing-and-put-the-launcher-icon-on-cobalt.md)):
  - from 41 px, an expression with open eyes, thinking or sad, blinks every 3 to 7 s, one blink in five double: the eyes shut in 70 ms behind an ink lid, hold 110 ms, 160 ms when sad, and open in 60 ms; a thinking one sometimes glances, its pupils moving up to 22 units across and 14 up or down for 1.4 s;
  - from 128 px, 홈's 140 among them ([ADR 0193](../adr/0193-make-home-one-screen-of-sky-over-ground-with-the-mascot-on-a-hill-the-streak-s-days-and-one-action.md)), thinking and sad also breathe, 1.2% wider and 1.8% shorter about the feet on 3 s; the happy one wobbles on 1.8 s, 1.1 s of squash, stretch and settle about its feet, which stay on the ground, its arms swinging about their shoulders, while its ground ticks stay still ([ADR 0185](../adr/0185-wobble-the-happy-mascot-in-place-with-its-ground-still-instead-of-the-hop.md)); the sleepy one breathes deeper on 4.2 s, its Z's drifting up and fading;
  - at 40 px or less, under reduce motion and off screen, it is the still image: the phone keeps the PNG and draws a moving mascot with a `CustomPainter` in its own layer, on one ticker that stops where a `TickerMode` is off or the app is in the background; the web keeps the `<img>` and draws a moving one inline, its `IntersectionObserver` telling it when it is on screen and how wide, and its `ResizeObserver` how wide it is drawn as that changes while it stays, so a mascot resized across 40 or 128 px takes the drawing of its new size, in either direction, as if it had first been drawn at it; the still image and the moving drawing take the same classes, so the box does not change across the swap (#627).

### Icons

- Icons are Lucide's, a 2 px stroke with rounded caps and joins: `lucide_icons_flutter` in the app, with the theme's back and close buttons drawn from it, and `lucide-react` on the web.
- An icon is drawn only where it carries meaning the words beside it do not, or where it is the platform's convention ([ADR 0116](../adr/0116-draw-an-icon-only-where-it-says-what-the-words-beside-it-do-not-or-follows-the-platform.md)):
  - it stays on the bottom tabs; back, close and search; play on a study button; each deck's own icon; the swipe hints' check and cross; a drag handle; the ⋯ menu; a chevron that leads on; a selection's check; and a progress indicator while work runs;
  - otherwise a control or a row is its words alone: no icon in a tinted square at the start of an option row, no icon inside a status chip, no icon next to a settings row's title, and none beside a status its words already name, such as "동기화됨";
  - an option row's second line stays only where its title alone does not say what happens: "사진 찍기" and "앨범에서 고르기" keep theirs, and "직접 입력" and "앞면 소리 자동 재생" have none;
  - the card editor names a block's type, its deck and its actions in words alone, and keeps the ⋯ menus and the label strip's chips ([ADR 0117](../adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md));
  - its add sheet, "칸 추가", is the one exception: each type's row is its icon, plain in `ink2` with no tinted square, its name and one line, on the phone and the web ([ADR 0171](../adr/0171-give-every-type-in-the-add-sheet-an-icon-and-a-line-and-keep-a-field-s-selection-through-a-scrolling-drag.md)).

## Rationale

- [ADR 0166](../adr/0166-draw-no-mascot-during-a-study-session-let-it-present-the-coach-with-a-back-and-a-next-and-a-tilted-hand-and-fill-an-empty-day-s-bar.md) records why no mascot is drawn during a study session, why it presents the coach with "이전" and "다음" and no "건너뛰기", and why the coach's touch is a pointing hand drawn in `tools/brand/` in the theme's tokens.
- [ADR 0154](../adr/0154-draw-the-logo-with-one-name-in-the-reader-s-language-korean-or-english.md) records why a lockup holds one name, why the web draws no knowno lockup, and why the stacked slot is 150 wide.
- [ADR 0153](../adr/0153-draw-the-lockups-in-cobalt-on-a-white-or-dark-card-circle.md) records why the lockups take the icon's cobalt on a white or dark card circle, and the dark splash's circle with them.
- [ADR 0152](../adr/0152-let-the-mascot-blink-and-breathe-from-its-own-drawing-and-put-the-launcher-icon-on-cobalt.md) records why the mascot moves from its own drawing rather than Rive, Lottie or a video, why a closed eye is a lid, the motion by expression and size, and the cobalt launcher icon with the splash on the screens' grounds.
- [ADR 0185](../adr/0185-wobble-the-happy-mascot-in-place-with-its-ground-still-instead-of-the-hop.md) records why the happy mascot wobbles in place instead of hopping, and why its ground stays still.
- [ADR 0193](../adr/0193-make-home-one-screen-of-sky-over-ground-with-the-mascot-on-a-hill-the-streak-s-days-and-one-action.md) records why the mascot breathes from 128 px rather than 200, so that 홈's 140 does.
- [ADR 0250](../adr/0250-draw-malangi-s-chat-as-a-messenger-with-a-profile-by-each-run-of-its-turns-three-dots-from-send-to-the-first-text-and-no-mascot-in-the-top-bar.md) records why both clients draw 말랑이 in a profile beside its bubbles and not in the chat's top bar.
- [ADR 0248](../adr/0248-meet-malangi-on-home-alone-offer-the-chat-once-a-day-in-a-bubble-over-the-mascot-and-open-it-naming-no-note.md) records why the phone draws no edge character and offers the chat from 홈's mascot by a bubble once a day.
- [ADR 0141](../adr/0141-polish-the-coral-mascot-make-coral-the-primary-action-and-place-its-expressions-on-both-clients.md) records the wider redraw, why 모른다 stays, where each expression appears, and the exception to ADR 0116 and the rule against decoration.
- [ADR 0139](../adr/0139-draw-both-clients-around-the-coral-mascot-with-warm-colour-tokens-and-rounder-taller-buttons.md) records how the marks are generated.
- [ADR 0134](../adr/0134-name-the-app-knowno-serve-it-at-knowno-app-identify-it-as-app-knowno-and-reach-the-backend-at-api-knowno-app.md) records the name.
- [ADR 0116](../adr/0116-draw-an-icon-only-where-it-says-what-the-words-beside-it-do-not-or-follows-the-platform.md) records where an icon is drawn and where words alone, and why the photo rows keep their second line.
- [ADR 0027](../adr/0027-draw-both-clients-in-the-smartman-brand-and-the-v2-screens.md) records the brand, the icons and the generated launcher icons and splash; issue #72 records them as the PM decided them on 2026-10-15.
