# Design checks

The tests and checks that hold the phone and the web to the design documents, each with what makes it fail.

## Current decisions

### Theme and tokens

- `app/test/design/theme_test.dart` fails on a colour outside the theme, Google's button and the mascot's generated drawing; on a spacing or font-size literal outside the theme, the components, Google's button and `CardText`; on a dialog, or a sheet opened other than through `showAppSheet`; on a Material chip under `app/lib/`; on a radius of the scale off its value, or a button or sheet the theme draws in another; on a body or caption role off the body's 16 or the caption's 13; on a text pair under 4.5:1 or a grade's chip under 3:1 on the ground; and on the switch's outline on the ground or its thumb on its track under 3:1, each in both themes. `web/src/styles/__tests__/contrast.test.ts` checks the web's ratios.
- `app/test/study/stats_view_test.dart` fails, in both themes, when two recall bands are less than 45 degrees apart in hue, a band but light 보통 is under 3:1 on the surface, or two adjacent heat steps differ in lightness by less than 1.5:1; `web/src/features/study/__tests__/record-shades.test.ts` checks the web's `/record` by the same rules ([`web-study.md`](../web-study.md)).
- `app/test/home/hero_test.dart` checks 홈's quiet text at 4.5:1 and a deck's progress bar on its track at 3:1 in both themes.
- `app/test/design/jua_coverage_test.dart` reads the fonts' tables against every string we write.
- `web/src/styles/__tests__/theme-tokens.test.ts` checks the `hatch` utility against `AppHatch`.
- On the web, `web/eslint.config.mjs` takes `web/src/design/class-rules.ts`'s selectors, so `make lint` fails on a colour, a type size, a radius at a token's value or a length written out in UI code, and, outside `web/src/design/`, on a job a design component draws written by hand: the button type, the red fill or the old red pair, a chip's radius and type together, a search input, a section's label, and a modal by `aria-modal`, a dialog role or a `<dialog>` that does not spread `useModalDialog`'s props ([`web.md`](../web.md) §Checks).

### Accessibility and layout

- `app/test/screenshots/screenshots_test.dart` draws every screen and sheet in both themes, in Korean at a phone's size (390 by 844), 홈 in each hero state and the 노트 tab with four notes on phones 667, 844 and 932 high, and sign-in, the first-run 홈, 홈 with no card, a new note, a note's cards, the card editor with its undo bar and its label presets, study past the day's plan, 기록 and photo import's unavailable state again at the largest text size, text scale 3.12, each named `-largest-text`, when run in `app/` with `flutter test --update-goldens --dart-define=SCREENSHOTS=true --dart-define-from-file=config/dev.json test/screenshots`; it writes them to `app/build/screenshots/`, with each one's text sizes in `type-sizes.txt` beside them, fails on a screen or sheet whose own chrome draws more than three sizes, and is skipped otherwise.
- `app/test/design/app_sheet_test.dart` lays out a Korean sheet title and body on a 390 wide phone and fails on a line that ends inside a word or on text a screen reader would not read as written, with a control of one word wider than the line that still wraps.
- `app/test/design/app_rows_test.dart` lays out an `AppListRow`'s Korean subtitle on a 390 wide phone at text scale 1.0 and 2.0, and fails on a line that ends inside a word or on text a screen reader would not read as written, with a control of one word wider than the line that still wraps; `app/test/home/deck_screen_test.dart` fails at both scales when the empty deck's line wraps or is not read as written, and `app/test/home/home_screen_test.dart` when a note's name in 홈's drawer breaks inside a word or is not read as written, against a one-word name that still wraps. Every test reading such text finds it through `shownText` in `app/test/word_breaks.dart`, which ignores the word joiners.
- `app/test/deck/whole_card_view_test.dart` fails when an empty blank draws a fill or its answer, or is not a gap of its answer's width over a `muted` underline, in both themes, against the filled blank on `brandTint`; when, on study's cards, an empty blank is not a box on the theme's hatch 2 em wide at its line's height whatever its answer, or holds its answer or an underline, in both themes, against a filled one's answer on the hatch; `web/src/features/decks/__tests__/whole-card.test.tsx` checks the same on the web, with a table's hidden cell, a photo's boxes and `faceLength`; and when a Korean cloze sentence, its blank included, breaks inside a word at a narrow width or is not read as written. `app/test/photo_import/failure_view_test.dart` fails when the refused-access message breaks inside a Korean word on a phone or is not read as written.

### Components

- `app/test/design/app_rows_test.dart` fails when a chip, status or action, selected or not, is not the fully round `chip` radius in the `section` type in its tone, when an action chip's space around it does not act or a disabled one does, when a pressed row draws a splash or no dim, when a row inside an `AppGroup` or a `SliverAppGroup` draws a surface of its own, or when the group's rows are not split by a hairline under all but the first, against a row alone that keeps its surface. `app/test/design/app_button_test.dart` checks the same press for a button, with a control that a splashing theme's circle is seen, and fails when a button gives a haptic of its own.
- The haptics are checked through a mock of the platform channel: a saved card's in `app/test/deck/card_editor_screen_test.dart`, against a refused save that gives none, and the destructive confirmation's in `app/test/design/app_sheet_test.dart`, against a cancel and a dismissal that give none.
- The sheet rule is checked where each sheet is tested: the duplicate sheet in `app/test/deck/card_editor_screen_test.dart` and the report sheet in `app/test/sharing/public_deck_screen_test.dart` have no cancel; the card delete confirmation in the former, the account deletion confirmation in `app/test/account/account_screen_test.dart`, and `showAppConfirm` in `app/test/design/app_sheet_test.dart`, put cancel above delete.
- `app/test/widget_test.dart` composes the app, opens a new card's editor and fails when a tap on the deck's name over the form, or a drag that scrolls the form, leaves the keyboard up, with controls that a tap on another field moves the focus to it, a tap on "뜻 추가" adds a meaning and a drag across the label strip keeps the keyboard; and when "앞면" loses its newline key or a label field's action key does not close the keyboard.

- The rule on icons is checked where each screen is tested: the deck chooser, the "사진 더 추가" sheet and the photo import bar in `app/test/photo_import/photo_import_screen_test.dart`, the settings rows in `app/test/account/account_screen_test.dart` and the sync status in `app/test/sync/sync_status_test.dart`; each fails when an icon or the badge it names is drawn, and the bar's check finds the chevron it keeps.
- Each mascot's place and expression is checked where its screen is tested: `app/test/home/home_screen_test.dart`, `app/test/home/deck_screen_test.dart`, `app/test/study/study_screen_test.dart`, which also checks none while a card is studied, the coach's thinking one beside its bubble, leaving with it, and the completion panel's alone, `app/test/photo_import/photo_import_screen_test.dart`, which also fails on one beside a failed row, and `app/test/photo_import/failure_view_test.dart`; on the web `today.test.tsx`, `deck.test.tsx`, `study.test.tsx` and `authoring-flow.test.tsx`.
- `app/test/design/mascot_test.dart` fails on an expression with no file at a density, or one a screen reader reaches; on a mascot that moves at 40 or under reduce motion, against a control that it moves with reduce motion off; on one whose ticker or next blink outlives it, ticks where its ticker mode is off or the app is in the background, or starts a blink while off; on a painted mascot at rest that differs from its PNG, against a control of another expression; and on a blink, a glance or a loop off ADR 0152's timings. On the web, `web/src/design/__tests__/brand.test.tsx` fails on a mascot that moves under reduce motion or keeps moving once it is turned on, on one that moves off its size's motion, and on one that leaves a timer or an observer off screen or once gone. - `scripts/run.sh web lint` runs `tools/brand/generate.mjs --check`, which fails when a brand SVG or a client's mascot parts differ from what the generator writes or a brand PNG differs by a mean of 1 in 255 or more.
- `app/test/account/sign_in_screen_test.dart` fails when the sign-in screen's logo is not the Korean lockup, read as 알아몰라, in a Korean locale, or not the English one, read as knowno, in an English or any other locale, in either theme.
- `app/test/launcher_assets_test.dart` fails when a launcher icon, Android's adaptive icon or a splash image is missing, is not the size its catalogue names, or does not draw its source scaled to that size, and when a splash ground is not its colour; a control tells the light icon from the dark one.
- `app/test/app_name_test.dart` fails when the English or Korean `appTitle` is not knowno or 알아몰라, when iOS's `InfoPlist.strings` or Android's `app_name` in either language differs from it, when the manifest's label is not `@string/app_name`, and when the Android namespace and application ID, `MainActivity`'s package or the iOS bundle IDs are not `app.knowno`.
