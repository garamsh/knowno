# Home

The app's `home/` shell: the signed-in screens that show more than one domain or lead from one domain into another, and how they compose the domains.

## Current decisions

### Surface and location

- The shell lives in `app/lib/home/`; its surface is `app/lib/home/home.dart`, which exports `HomeScreen`, with internals under `app/lib/home/src/` ([ADR 0039](adr/0039-name-every-top-level-folder-by-its-responsibility-compose-across-domains-in-a-home-shell-and-keep-seams-off-screens.md), [`structure.md`](structure.md)):
  - `home_screen.dart` — `HomeScreen`: the 기록, 홈 and 노트 tabs and their bar, 노트's own navigator, 설정 over the menu, how the photo import bar opens the import, and the offer to resume a photo import;
  - `home_tab.dart` — 홈: one screen of the sky over the ground in every state, the first run's included, and how the sky shrinks to its content and the page scrolls where the two need more than the screen;
  - `streak_days.dart` — `StreakDays`, 홈's streak: "n일 연속" over a dot a day for the last seven study days;
  - `notes_tab.dart` — `NotesTab`, the 노트 tab: its title, 새 노트 and its ⋯, the search and every deck's row, over `assistant`'s edge character;
  - `record_tab.dart` — the 기록 tab: its title over `study`'s record, over the edge character;
  - `deck_screen.dart` — `DeckScreen`, a deck's page;
  - `new_deck.dart` — `openNewDeck`, which opens `deck`'s 새 노트 and then the deck it made on the 노트 tab;
  - `list_header.dart` — the header form the 노트 tab and the deck screen share: `ListHeaderActions`, the screen's tonal "+" action, where it has one, and its ⋯, and the height of a top bar holding them; the always-shown search under it is `app/lib/design/`'s `AppSearchField`, and "카드 추가"'s sheet of options its `showAppChoice`;
  - `above_menu.dart` — `aboveMenu`, the context whose navigator shows a screen or sheet over the menu, and `RaisedIconButton`, the top bars' icon buttons;
  - `open_assistant.dart` — `openAssistant`, which opens `assistant`'s chat over the menu, from a note when given one, and gives it the callback that opens a note it added: a sync, then `DeckScreen`, without the edge character, over the chat;
  - `progress_bar.dart` — a deck's progress bar;
- The composition site passes `HomeScreen` to `account`'s `AccountGate` as the screen it opens while an account is signed in ([`account.md`](account.md)). Nothing else imports `home/`.

### Composition

- `home/` sits above every domain: it imports the domains' surfaces and the foundations, and builds each screen from the domains' exported widgets, handing values and callbacks between them, as a web route does.
- It holds no reads and no rules of its own: it declares no provider and imports no database. What a screen shows, it reads through a domain's providers and widgets.
- The tabs compose:
  - `deck`'s `decksProvider`, `cardCountsProvider`, `DeckRow`, `DeckTile` and `NewDeckScreen`, for the decks, their rows and a new deck ([`deck.md`](deck.md));
  - `study`'s `TodayOverviewReader`, for the sky's count, minutes, backlog and next review and the streak and its last seven study days; `TodayCounter`, for each deck's count; `ProgressCounter`, for each deck's progress; `StatsView`, the 기록 tab's content, given the tab's title; `openStudy`, through which every study button opens a `StudyScreen`; and `FirstDailyMinutes` and `DailyMinutesRow`, the daily study minutes' first-run step and settings row ([`study.md`](study.md), [ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md));
  - `account`'s `ProfileButton`, the account's initial, which opens `account`'s `AccountScreen`, 설정, over the menu, given `sync`'s `SyncStatusView` as the status it shows and, as its study settings' rows, `study`'s `DailyMinutesRow`, `study`'s `AutoplayAudioRow` and `deck`'s `PartOfSpeechPresetsRow` ([`account.md`](account.md), [`sync.md`](sync.md), [ADR 0165](adr/0165-open-on-today-over-the-last-seven-days-move-the-notes-to-a-tab-and-open-settings-from-the-profile-button.md));
  - `sharing`'s `sharingServiceProvider`, for the browse screen ([`sharing.md`](sharing.md));
  - `assistant`'s `assistantClientProvider` and `assistantReadyCardsProvider`, for the mascot's entry and its bubble, and `AssistantChatScreen`, given the callback that opens a note, with `sync`'s `syncControllerProvider` for the sync before it opens ([`assistant-app.md`](assistant-app.md));
  - `photo_import`'s `photoImportsProvider`, `PhotoImports.opener`, `openPhotoImport` and `offerPhotoImportResume`, for the import under way ([`ai-extraction.md`](ai-extraction.md)).
- The deck screen composes `deck`'s `deckProvider`, `cardsProvider`, `CardBackRow`, `CardEditorScreen`, `showNameSheet`, `showDeckAppearanceSheet` and `DeckRepository`; `study`'s `TodayCounter`, `ProgressCounter`, `openStudy` and `CardPreviewScreen`, which it hands `CardEditorScreen` as its preview ([ADR 0168](adr/0168-draw-the-card-editor-s-preview-with-study-s-own-faces-at-study-s-size-exported-by-study-and-handed-to-the-editor-by-home.md)); `sharing`'s `sharingServiceProvider` and `onlineProvider`, for publishing; and `photo_import`'s `photoImportProvider`.
- A `DeckRow` takes what it shows of today and of its progress as widgets, which the 노트 tab builds from `TodayCounter` and `ProgressCounter`: the deck's places for what `study` draws ([`structure.md`](structure.md)).

### Tabs

- The signed-in app is three tabs, 기록, 홈 and 노트, with 홈 in the middle, in a bar floating on its edge at the foot, each its name alone, the current one in `accent`, each read as a selectable button ([ADR 0165](adr/0165-open-on-today-over-the-last-seven-days-move-the-notes-to-a-tab-and-open-settings-from-the-profile-button.md)). The app opens on 홈. 설정 is not a tab: 홈's profile button opens it over the menu.
- 기록 is built the first time it is shown, since its read folds every review through the engine; 노트 is built from the first, so a deck 홈 or a photo import opens has its navigator; after that every tab keeps its state while another is shown.
- 노트 has a navigator of its own inside its tab, so a deck's screen opens above the menu; a deck 홈 opens, 새 노트 makes or a photo import saves into opens there, showing the tab. Study, the card editor, 새 노트, the browse screen, 설정, every sheet and the settings' own screens open on the app's navigator, over the menu, through `aboveMenu`.
- Back on 노트 first closes what 노트's navigator opened; on 기록 or 노트's first screen it returns to 홈; on 홈 it is left to the platform. A tap on 노트 while it shows a deck returns to 노트's first screen.
- While a photo import is under way, `photo_import`'s bar heads every screen, the tabs and every screen over them, study included; the composition site places it over the app's navigator, and every screen lays out below it ([ADR 0183](adr/0183-keep-a-photo-import-reading-over-every-screen-with-one-bar-at-the-top-on-both-clients.md)). It reads "사진 읽는 중 k/n", "사진 n장을 읽지 못했어요", "카드 n장 확인하기" or "읽던 사진 k장 이어서 읽기". While it is built, the home screen sets `PhotoImports.opener`, so a tap opens the import over whatever screen shows, and an import that saves opens the deck it saved into on 노트, under any screen still over the tabs; one left for "직접 입력" or "엑셀·CSV 파일" where photo import is unavailable opens its note on 노트, with the editor or the spreadsheet import over it, so back returns to the note (#606) ([ADR 0094](adr/0094-read-a-photo-import-of-up-to-fifty-photos-three-at-a-time-as-an-app-level-job-kept-on-the-device-and-offered-again-after-a-restart.md)).
- On opening, when a closed app left a photo import with photos unread, the home screen offers to resume it, "읽던 사진 k장을 이어서 읽을까요?", once the import is read back.

### 홈

- 홈 is one screen with no scroll ([ADR 0193](adr/0193-make-home-one-screen-of-sky-over-ground-with-the-mascot-on-a-hill-the-streak-s-days-and-one-action.md)): the sky, on the `hero` surface with its words in `onHero`, over the ground, joined by a hill. The sky runs up behind the status bar, whose glyphs are light over it and the theme's on every other screen (`AppStatusBar`, the composition site's root region and 홈's own); it takes 58% of the screen where the ground's content leaves it room and shrinks to its own content where not. Where the two need more than the screen, at the largest text sizes, the page scrolls as one, and nothing is cut.
- The sky: the app's name, and at its right the profile button, the account's initial in Pretendard on `heroButton`, named "설정"; then, centred in what the mascot leaves, its words. At its foot the ground rises in a quadratic hill 32 high at its crest, and the mascot stands on the crest at `mascotHero` (140) in every state and on every phone, thinking while cards are left or nothing is done yet and sleepy once the day is done; where the app has an assistant it is a button named "말랑이와 대화하기" that opens the chat over the menu, and while a proposal of new cards is ready, a speech bubble over its head reads "카드 n장 만들어 뒀어요" and opens the chat too ([`assistant-app.md`](assistant-app.md), [ADR 0201](adr/0201-chat-with-malangi-on-the-phone-in-an-assistant-domain-kept-in-the-app-s-preferences-and-opened-from-home-s-mascot.md)). The bubble is laid out in the sky above the mascot, so a larger text size grows the sky rather than cover its words.
- The ground: what the day shows, centred, over the one action at the cta size on its edge. By the day ([`study.md`](study.md)):
  - with cards left: "오늘", "n장" in `heroFigure`, "약 m분이면 끝나요"; the streak; the primary "시작";
  - in a backlog: "다시 시작하면 돼요" and "밀린 복습 n장을 급한 것부터 해요. 새 카드는 잠시 쉬어요."; the streak; "시작";
  - with the day's plan done: "오늘", "다 했어요" in `heroFigure` and the cards answered today, "n장", or, where none was answered, "다음 복습은 내일 · n장, 약 m분", or the weekday, or "앞으로 7일 동안 복습할 카드가 없어요"; the streak, today's dot filled; the secondary "학습하기", which goes on past today's cards ([ADR 0049](adr/0049-study-in-one-flow-that-goes-on-past-today-s-cards-as-recorded-study-with-the-current-card-showing-its-front-alone.md));
  - while no deck holds a card: "첫 카드를 만들어 볼까요?" and, with one deck, "카드 추가", which opens the deck on 노트 with its add sheet up, or, with several, "노트에서 카드 추가", which shows the 노트 tab, and no streak ([ADR 0066](adr/0066-open-a-deck-made-by-hand-and-lead-an-empty-deck-and-a-cardless-today-tab-to-adding-cards.md));
  - before the first deck: "첫 노트를 만들어 볼까요?" and, where the app has an assistant, "말랑이와 첫 노트 만들기", which opens the chat, or, where it has none, "새 노트 만들기", which opens 새 노트, and, with sharing, "공개 노트 둘러보기" as a secondary, and no streak ([ADR 0150](adr/0150-open-on-a-home-of-today-on-the-hero-with-the-notes-in-a-drawer-keep-the-menu-on-a-note-and-make-a-note-on-a-full-screen.md));
  - while the count cannot be read: no words in the sky, and the reason in a `surface` row in the streak's place over "시작", since it asks the user to act; while it is read, the mascot alone.
- "시작" and "학습하기" open one session over every deck, whatever the count ([ADR 0040](adr/0040-keep-every-study-button-enabled-at-zero-cards-left.md)). 홈 holds no note: the 노트 tab's rows carry each deck's count for today, and 기록 has the record.
- The first-run step: once 홈 shows a day with cards, while it is the selected tab and no screen is open over it, the daily study minutes' sheet opens once per device as "하루 몇 분 학습할까요?", with "건너뛰기", unless minutes are already stored from any device ([ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md)). Every study button opens its session through `study`'s `openStudy`, which first shows that step where it has not shown.
- The streak: "n일 연속" in `title`, n in `accent`, over a dot a day for the last seven study days, today's last: a day with cards filled in `accent`, a day without one outlined in `outline`, today outlined in `todayMark` and filled in it once the day's plan is done, each over its weekday in `micro`, today's "오늘" in `section` and `todayText`, a label on one line shrunk to its column where it is wider; read as one image naming each day and its cards. It counts what 기록 counts, `study`'s `TodayOverview.week`. It is drawn whenever any deck holds a card, a week with no study included, and not while none does ([ADR 0193](adr/0193-make-home-one-screen-of-sky-over-ground-with-the-mascot-on-a-hill-the-streak-s-days-and-one-action.md)). The bar chart and its "지난 7일 n장" are 기록's.
- The edge character ([`assistant-app.md`](assistant-app.md), [ADR 0204](adr/0204-draw-malangi-at-a-screen-s-edge-name-the-note-a-chat-opens-from-attach-photos-to-a-message-and-follow-their-job-from-the-phone.md)): the 노트 tab, a note's screen and 기록 draw `assistant`'s `EdgeAssistant` over their body, their lists keeping `edgeReserve` at their foot, 기록 through `StatsView`'s `bottomInset`; a note opens the chat with itself as its context, the other two with none. Where the app has an assistant, adding by hand is in a ⋯: the 노트 tab's ⋯, "노트 탭 메뉴", holds "직접 추가", which opens 새 노트, before "공개 노트 둘러보기" and is there without sharing, and the "+ 새 노트" below is not drawn; the note's ⋯ holds "직접 추가", which opens the add sheet, first, and its "+ 카드 추가" is not drawn. Where it has none, both stay as described below.
- The 노트 tab: "노트" in `deckTitle`, read as a heading, with at its right "+ 새 노트", tonal at the header size, which opens 새 노트, and, with sharing, ⋯, "노트 탭 메뉴", whose one item, "공개 노트 둘러보기", opens the browse screen; with sharing off there is no ⋯ ([ADR 0172](adr/0172-make-a-note-with-the-choices-of-its-customization-head-the-notes-and-a-note-with-a-tonal-action-and-a-menu-over-an-always-shown-search.md)). Under them, while there is any deck, the "노트 검색" field, always shown, which keeps the decks whose name holds it, ignoring case, with "찾는 노트가 없어요. 다른 단어로 검색해 보세요." when none does; then every deck's row, and nothing under the rows.
- A count that cannot be read is a row on the ground over its action, with its reason.
- Each deck is a `DeckRow` ([`deck.md`](deck.md)), with a brand-tint "오늘 n" chip when a session over it would study n > 0 cards now, under its name "n장 중 m장 익힘", and under that a progress bar, the mastered cards in `accent` and the learning ones quieter. The learning count is not in the text: a screen reader reads the counts from the bar's label, "익힘 m장, 학습 중 l장, 새 카드 k장", in the row's node ([ADR 0096](adr/0096-state-a-deck-s-mastered-of-all-cards-under-its-name-over-its-bar-on-the-row-and-the-deck-page-and-an-empty-deck-as-0-cards.md)). A deck with no card shows "0장" and no bar, and a deck shows "n장" alone while its progress is read. A count that cannot be read shows an icon whose tooltip says why. A tap opens the deck's screen above the menu.

### 새 노트

### The record tab

### The deck screen

### Interface

- Every string the screens show comes from the shared ARB set, and every tap target carries a semantic label: its text, or a tooltip for an icon button.
- The screens follow the principles and the theme of [`design/`](design/principles.md).
