# Home

The app's `home/` shell: the signed-in screens that show more than one domain or lead from one domain into another, and how they compose the domains.

## Current decisions

### Surface and location

- The shell lives in `app/lib/home/`; its surface is `app/lib/home/home.dart`, which exports `HomeScreen`, with internals under `app/lib/home/src/` ([ADR 0039](adr/0039-name-every-top-level-folder-by-its-responsibility-compose-across-domains-in-a-home-shell-and-keep-seams-off-screens.md), [`structure.md`](structure.md)):
  - `home_screen.dart` — `HomeScreen`: the 기록, 홈 and 노트 tabs and their bar, 노트's own navigator, 설정 over the menu, the photo import bar and the offer to resume a photo import;
  - `home_tab.dart` — 홈: the first-run home before the first deck, then the hero over the week card, fitted to the screen;
  - `notes_tab.dart` — `NotesTab`, the 노트 tab: its title, 새 노트, the search and every deck's row;
  - `first_run.dart` — `FirstRun`, the layout of how to start that the first-run home uses;
  - `record_tab.dart` — the 기록 tab: its title over `study`'s record;
  - `deck_screen.dart` — `DeckScreen`, a deck's page;
  - `new_deck.dart` — `openNewDeck`, which opens `deck`'s 새 노트 and then the deck it made on the 노트 tab;
  - `option_sheet.dart` — the sheet of options "카드 추가" opens;
  - `above_menu.dart` — `aboveMenu`, the context whose navigator shows a screen or sheet over the menu, and `RaisedIconButton`, the top bars' icon buttons;
  - `progress_bar.dart` — a deck's progress bar;
- The composition site passes `HomeScreen` to `account`'s `AccountGate` as the screen it opens while an account is signed in ([`account.md`](account.md)). Nothing else imports `home/`.

### Composition

- `home/` sits above every domain: it imports the domains' surfaces and the foundations, and builds each screen from the domains' exported widgets, handing values and callbacks between them, as a web route does.
- It holds no reads and no rules of its own: it declares no provider and imports no database. What a screen shows, it reads through a domain's providers and widgets.
- The tabs compose:
  - `deck`'s `decksProvider`, `cardCountsProvider`, `DeckRow`, `DeckTile` and `NewDeckScreen`, for the decks, their rows and a new deck ([`deck.md`](deck.md));
  - `study`'s `TodayOverviewReader`, for the hero's count, minutes, streak, backlog and next review and the week card's last seven study days; `TodayCounter`, for each deck's count; `ProgressCounter`, for each deck's progress; `StatsView`, the 기록 tab's content, given the tab's title; `openStudy`, through which every study button opens a `StudyScreen`; and `FirstDailyMinutes` and `DailyMinutesRow`, the daily study minutes' first-run step and settings row ([`study.md`](study.md), [ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md));
  - `account`'s `ProfileButton`, the account's initial, which opens `account`'s `AccountScreen`, 설정, over the menu, given `sync`'s `SyncStatusView` as the status it shows and, as its study settings' rows, `study`'s `DailyMinutesRow`, `study`'s `AutoplayAudioRow` and `deck`'s `PartOfSpeechPresetsRow` ([`account.md`](account.md), [`sync.md`](sync.md), [ADR 0165](adr/0165-open-on-today-over-the-last-seven-days-move-the-notes-to-a-tab-and-open-settings-from-the-profile-button.md));
  - `sharing`'s `sharingServiceProvider`, for the browse screen ([`sharing.md`](sharing.md));
  - `photo_import`'s `photoImportsProvider`, `PhotoImportBar`, `openPhotoImport` and `offerPhotoImportResume`, for the import under way ([`ai-extraction.md`](ai-extraction.md)).
- The deck screen composes `deck`'s `deckProvider`, `cardsProvider`, `CardBackRow`, `CardEditorScreen`, `showNameSheet`, `showDeckAppearanceSheet` and `DeckRepository`; `study`'s `TodayCounter`, `ProgressCounter` and `openStudy`; `sharing`'s `sharingServiceProvider` and `onlineProvider`, for publishing; and `photo_import`'s `photoImportProvider`.
- A `DeckRow` takes what it shows of today and of its progress as widgets, which the 노트 tab builds from `TodayCounter` and `ProgressCounter`: the deck's places for what `study` draws ([`structure.md`](structure.md)).

### Tabs

- The signed-in app is three tabs, 기록, 홈 and 노트, with 홈 in the middle, in a bar floating on its edge at the foot, each its name alone, the current one in `accent`, each read as a selectable button ([ADR 0165](adr/0165-open-on-today-over-the-last-seven-days-move-the-notes-to-a-tab-and-open-settings-from-the-profile-button.md)). The app opens on 홈. 설정 is not a tab: 홈's profile button opens it over the menu.
- 기록 is built the first time it is shown, since its read folds every review through the engine; 노트 is built from the first, so a deck 홈 or a photo import opens has its navigator; after that every tab keeps its state while another is shown.
- 노트 has a navigator of its own inside its tab, so a deck's screen opens above the menu; a deck 홈 opens, 새 노트 makes or a photo import saves into opens there, showing the tab. Study, the card editor, 새 노트, the browse screen, 설정, every sheet and the settings' own screens open on the app's navigator, over the menu, through `aboveMenu`.
- Back on 노트 first closes what 노트's navigator opened; on 기록 or 노트's first screen it returns to 홈; on 홈 it is left to the platform. A tap on 노트 while it shows a deck returns to 노트's first screen.
- While a photo import is under way, `photo_import`'s bar sits above the tab bar: "사진 읽는 중 k/n", "카드 n장 확인하기" or "읽던 사진 k장 이어서 읽기". A tap opens the import, and an import that saves opens the deck it saved into on 노트 ([ADR 0094](adr/0094-read-a-photo-import-of-up-to-fifty-photos-three-at-a-time-as-an-app-level-job-kept-on-the-device-and-offered-again-after-a-restart.md)).
- On opening, when a closed app left a photo import with photos unread, the home screen offers to resume it, "읽던 사진 k장을 이어서 읽을까요?", once the import is read back.

### 홈

- Before the first deck, the first-run home, over the menu: the app's name with the profile button at its right, the thinking mascot, "첫 노트를 만들어 볼까요?", "새 노트 만들기", the one primary, which opens 새 노트, and, with sharing, "공개 노트 둘러보기" as a quiet button ([ADR 0150](adr/0150-open-on-a-home-of-today-on-the-hero-with-the-notes-in-a-drawer-keep-the-menu-on-a-note-and-make-a-note-on-a-full-screen.md)).
- After it, the first screen is today on the `hero` surface, rounded at its foot, its words in `onHero`: the app's name, and at its right the profile button, the account's initial in Pretendard on `heroButton`, named "설정"; then the mascot, the words, and the one action in `heroButton`, centred as one group. Under the hero, on the ground, the week card ([ADR 0165](adr/0165-open-on-today-over-the-last-seven-days-move-the-notes-to-a-tab-and-open-settings-from-the-profile-button.md)). The week card and the top bar keep their heights and the hero takes the rest: its words and action keep their sizes, and the mascot, square, takes what they leave, from `mascotHero` down to `mascotFloor`; only once it is at that floor does 홈 scroll, the hero and the week card together. By the day ([`study.md`](study.md)):
  - with cards left: the happy mascot, "오늘 n장" in `heroFigure`, "약 m분이면 끝나요" and "시작";
  - in a backlog: the thinking mascot, "다시 시작하면 돼요", "밀린 복습 n장을 급한 것부터 해요. 새 카드는 잠시 쉬어요." and "시작";
  - with the day's plan done: the happy mascot, "오늘 할 카드를 다 했어요", "다음 복습은 내일 · n장, 약 m분", or the weekday, or "앞으로 7일 동안 복습할 카드가 없어요", and "학습하기", which goes on past today's cards ([ADR 0049](adr/0049-study-in-one-flow-that-goes-on-past-today-s-cards-as-recorded-study-with-the-current-card-showing-its-front-alone.md));
  - while no deck holds a card: the happy mascot, "첫 카드를 만들어 볼까요?" and, with one deck, "카드 추가", which opens the deck on 노트 with its add sheet up, or, with several, "노트에서 카드 추가", which shows the 노트 tab ([ADR 0066](adr/0066-open-a-deck-made-by-hand-and-lead-an-empty-deck-and-a-cardless-today-tab-to-adding-cards.md));
  - while the count cannot be read: the mascot and "시작", the reason in a row in the week card's place, since it asks the user to act; while it is read, the mascot alone.
- "시작" and "학습하기" open one session over every deck, whatever the count ([ADR 0040](adr/0040-keep-every-study-button-enabled-at-zero-cards-left.md)). 홈 holds no note: the 노트 tab's rows carry each deck's count for today, and 기록 has the record.
- The first-run step: once 홈 shows a day with cards, while it is the selected tab and no screen is open over it, the daily study minutes' sheet opens once per device as "하루 몇 분 학습할까요?", with "건너뛰기", unless minutes are already stored from any device ([ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md)). Every study button opens its session through `study`'s `openStudy`, which first shows that step where it has not shown.
- The week card is a `surface` card over its edge: "n일 연속" in `title`, n in `accent`, with "지난 7일 n장" at its right; then a bar a day for the last seven study days, today's last, each under its count and over its weekday, today's in `accent` and the others in `heat1`, a day with no card a dashed outline, read as one image naming each day and its cards. It counts what 기록 counts, `study`'s `TodayOverview.week`. It is drawn whenever any deck holds a card, a week with no study included, and not while none does ([ADR 0165](adr/0165-open-on-today-over-the-last-seven-days-move-the-notes-to-a-tab-and-open-settings-from-the-profile-button.md)).
- The 노트 tab: "노트" in `deckTitle`, read as a heading, with "새 노트", a chip, which opens 새 노트; under them, while there is any deck, the "노트 검색" field, which keeps the decks whose name holds it, ignoring case, with "찾는 노트가 없어요. 다른 단어로 검색해 보세요." when none does; every deck's row; and under the rows, with sharing, "공개 노트 둘러보기", a text button, which opens the browse screen.
- A count that cannot be read is a row in the week card's place, with its reason.
- Each deck is a `DeckRow` ([`deck.md`](deck.md)), with a brand-tint "오늘 n" chip when a session over it would study n > 0 cards now, under its name "n장 중 m장 익힘", and under that a progress bar, the mastered cards in `accent` and the learning ones quieter. The learning count is not in the text: a screen reader reads the counts from the bar's label, "익힘 m장, 학습 중 l장, 새 카드 k장", in the row's node ([ADR 0096](adr/0096-state-a-deck-s-mastered-of-all-cards-under-its-name-over-its-bar-on-the-row-and-the-deck-page-and-an-empty-deck-as-0-cards.md)). A deck with no card shows "0장" and no bar, and a deck shows "n장" alone while its progress is read. A count that cannot be read shows an icon whose tooltip says why. A tap opens the deck's screen above the menu.

### 새 노트

- "새 노트" and "새 노트 만들기" open `deck`'s `NewDeckScreen` over the menu ([`deck.md`](deck.md)); "저장" then opens the deck it made, and "저장하고 카드 추가" opens it with its add sheet up, on the 노트 tab's navigator ([ADR 0150](adr/0150-open-on-a-home-of-today-on-the-hero-with-the-notes-in-a-drawer-keep-the-menu-on-a-note-and-make-a-note-on-a-full-screen.md), [ADR 0066](adr/0066-open-a-deck-made-by-hand-and-lead-an-empty-deck-and-a-cardless-today-tab-to-adding-cards.md)). ### The record tab

- The tab is `study`'s `StatsView` over every deck, given "기록" at 26/800, read as a heading, as the title it shows alone above its charts ([`study.md`](study.md)). There is no Plus badge: gating waits for the subscription issue (issue #90).

### The deck screen

- It opens above the menu. The top bar has a back button, the deck's name in `deckName`, read as a heading, and two raised icon buttons, each at least 44 square: a magnifier, "카드 검색", which opens a search field in the bar with "검색 닫기", and ⋯, "노트 설정", whose menu renames the deck, opens "노트 꾸미기" on it, whose "저장" stores the icon and colour and the photo "사진 넣기" chose or removed ([ADR 0107](adr/0107-store-media-in-a-private-supabase-storage-bucket-named-by-media-id-and-give-each-deck-an-image.md), [ADR 0103](adr/0103-give-each-deck-an-icon-and-a-colour-stored-as-two-unchecked-keys-and-drawn-on-its-tile-in-place-of-the-initial.md)), with sharing offers "노트 공개" or "비공개로 전환", disabled with a one-line reason while offline, and deletes it, "노트 삭제" in the destructive text colour; deleting returns to the 노트 tab's first screen ([ADR 0150](adr/0150-open-on-a-home-of-today-on-the-hero-with-the-notes-in-a-drawer-keep-the-menu-on-a-note-and-make-a-note-on-a-full-screen.md)). While the search is open, the top of the list steps aside, so the matches start under the pinned row, above a phone's keyboard; the system back closes the search and keeps the screen, and a second back leaves it. The name is the deck's as stored now, so a rename made on another device and synced in shows at once, in the title, the rename sheet, the delete question and the editor alike.
- At the top, while the deck holds any card, the primary "이 노트 학습 · n장", "이 노트 학습" while the count is pending or cannot be read, which opens a session over the deck whatever the count ([ADR 0040](adr/0040-keep-every-study-button-enabled-at-zero-cards-left.md)). There is no tile and no today card. While the deck holds no card, there is no study button at all ([ADR 0066](adr/0066-open-a-deck-made-by-hand-and-lead-an-empty-deck-and-a-cardless-today-tab-to-adding-cards.md)): the sleepy mascot, "아직 카드가 없어요. 카드를 추가해 시작해 보세요." and the primary "카드 추가" are the screen's content.
- Above the cards, while the deck has any, a row pinned under the top bar as the list scrolls (issue #142): "카드 n", read as a heading, the screen's one count of its cards, with "m장 익힘" once read and "공개" while the deck is published ([ADR 0118](adr/0118-name-each-concept-once-write-each-role-in-one-form-hold-every-screen-to-one-set-of-visual-details-and-redraw-settings-and-the-deck-screen-by-them.md)); and at its right "뒷면 보기" and a switch, read together as "뒷면 보기" with the switch's state (#451).
- The cards are one `SliverAppGroup`, each a `CardBackRow` split from the next by a hairline: its front alone, or, while the switch is on, its back muted after it, a card holding a blank drawn by its 칸 in place; a hidden back is drawn as nothing ([`deck.md`](deck.md), [ADR 0118](adr/0118-name-each-concept-once-write-each-role-in-one-form-hold-every-screen-to-one-set-of-visual-details-and-redraw-settings-and-the-deck-screen-by-them.md)). The switch is off each time the screen opens; it is not remembered, and the search's results follow it.
- A tap on a card row opens the card in the editor, and a screen reader hears the row as one button with the hint "카드 편집"; there is no pencil, long-press or per-row toggle. The search keeps the cards any of whose 칸, revealed, holds it, ignoring case ([ADR 0137](adr/0137-frame-a-card-by-its-blocks-each-block-type-deciding-whether-it-has-a-front-and-a-back.md)).
- The foot holds one "카드 추가", fixed above the menu as the list scrolls, the surface on its edge, or in the primary fill while the deck holds no card. It opens the sheet "카드 추가" over the menu, its rows their names alone: "직접 입력", which opens the editor, with photo import "사진 찍기" and "앨범에서 고르기" ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-start-a-deck-or-a-card-from-one-sheet-that-holds-the-photo-source-and-name-public-decks-once.md)), and "엑셀·CSV 파일", which adds cards from a spreadsheet to this deck ([`deck.md`](deck.md), [ADR 0150](adr/0150-open-on-a-home-of-today-on-the-hero-with-the-notes-in-a-drawer-keep-the-menu-on-a-note-and-make-a-note-on-a-full-screen.md)). A photo import that saved into another deck replaces this screen with that deck's; one into this deck returns here.

### Interface

- Every string the screens show comes from the shared ARB set, and every tap target carries a semantic label: its text, or a tooltip for an icon button.
- The screens follow the principles and the theme of [`design.md`](design.md), with `design`'s `BottomActions` as the deck screen's bottom bar.

## Rationale

- [ADR 0165](adr/0165-open-on-today-over-the-last-seven-days-move-the-notes-to-a-tab-and-open-settings-from-the-profile-button.md) records the menu 기록 / 홈 / 노트, 설정 from the profile button, 홈 as today over the week card with no drawer, the fit of the hero to the screen from `mascotHero` to `mascotFloor`, when the week card is drawn, and the 노트 tab; issues #610 and #611 record the owner's report from an iPhone 13 Pro and choice of option B', and the PM's answers on #611 the cardless rule.
